# P06.T28.g: records as built

The as-built records of P06.T28.g, moved from the Risks and open points of
[06-stellar-stage.md](../../06-stellar-stage.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **Deviations in P06.T28.g, as built (lane `p06-t24b-t28g`, 2026-10-31; at version 24, behind
  `STAR_EVENTS_VERSION` = 26, no protocol bump: `active_events` is optional).** `golden_diff` at
  24: no golden file changed.
  - _The hold._ `StellarRules::star_events` (`StarEventRules { Held, Composed }`,
    `stellar/events/mod.rs`), chosen in one `match` in `system::star_summary`. Tests:
    `stellar/rules/events_hold.rs`; the server's `from_26_the_running_events_and_their_light_are_sent`
    reaches the 26 arm through `Galaxy::from_params_at_version` (new, `testing` feature only).
  - _API._ In `stellar::events`: `StarEvent` (kind, ID, onset, duration, `outburst_class`,
    `luminosity_watts`), `StarEventKind` (the wire's seven), `StarEvents` (each kind's model built
    once; `events_in`, `active_at`, `at`), `EventEffects`, and the free `events_in` and
    `active_at`, all by onset, then kind, then ID. Model-level listings, which move nothing:
    `FlareModel::flares_active_at` (the assembly lists flares with P12.T5.e's
    `FlareModel::flares_in`), `FuOrionisModel::outbursts_in` and `outbursts_active_at`,
    `GlitchModel::{glitches_in, glitches_active_at}`,
    `GlitchCycle::{of_model, large_glitches_in, large_glitch_active_at}`, and
    `glitches::LARGE_GLITCH_DURATION`. `StarSummary` gains `active_events()` and
    `event_effects()` and is no longer `Copy`.
  - _Composed (the kinds' records' leans)._ L = min(L(t) + Σ ΔLᵢ, max(`L_Edd`, L(t))) + ΔL of a
    giant eruption, ΔL over the active flares (F − 1 against the epoch's L), outbursts and thermal
    pulse (F − 1 against L(t)), `L_Edd` at X = 0.76 − 3Z; R × √F in a pulse's transient; the spin
    offset of the Crab-like glitches and the Vela-like sawtooth. A magnetar's bursts and giant flares
    are listed with `luminosity_watts`, never added. The summary's state stays the track's, and so
    the brief (design note 14).
  - _Leans (2026-10-31)._ A Vela-like glitch lasts its recovery time, `τ_d` = 3.1 d (the Crab-like
    `RECOVERY_TIME_DAYS`, the model's only `τ_d`); a Crab-like one `RECOVERY_SPAN`, to its offset's
    end. `OutburstClass` is on `StarEvent` only. A star its pair has changed takes no events
    (`active_events` `None`). From 26 the wire's `luminosity_lsun` and `radius_rsun` are the
    composed ones; `teff_k`, `absolute_v_mag` and the pulsar's period stay the track's. The thermal
    pulses' model is built once per summary and shared with P06.T24.b's C/O
    (`system::Pulses::Built`).
  - _Agreement._ `alerts::local::tests::the_stellar_assembly_lists_the_local_events_kinds`: over
    both alert scenes, `events_in` lists exactly the flares, outbursts and glitches their kinds
    list, and holds every one `local_events` detects.
  - _Signatures._ The free `events_in(star, seed, body, window, out)` and
    `active_at(star, seed, body, t, out)` take the seed and the body, which Provides' sketch lacks:
    `StarModel` holds neither, and each kind's series is opened from them. Plan 12 reads this form.
  - _Magnitudes._ `StarEvent` carries an outburst's class and a magnetar event's luminosity only;
    a flare's energy, a glitch's size, an eruption's peak and a pulse's factor are regenerated from
    the ID by their kind's module. `StarEventDto` is unchanged (kind, onset, `duration_s`).
  - _Not done._ Magnetar episodes are not events (no wire kind). A star whose thermally pulsing AGB
    ends mid-transient steps there in L and R (P06.T28.f's 0.071 dex), untapered. The count test
    runs over 10⁴ bodies of one star per kind; bursts are checked against their module's listing,
    and thermal pulses and Vela-like glitches count whole cycles, within one a body. The
    `stellar/summaries` writer prints no events, so at 26 it pins none: the bump writes them.
  - _Movers at 26_ (a bless of the default suites with both holds forced on, GENERATOR_VERSION
    24): one golden, the server's `scene_systems` (6 `system_bodies` answers gain
    `active_events`); two server tests that assert its absence,
    `convert::stellar::tests::what_is_not_computed_is_absent_and_what_is_lacking_is_null` and
    `system_summary::pinned_summaries_are_the_sims_and_refusals_name_their_field`; and, in
    `rules::events_hold`, `the_star_events_are_held_only_until_the_26_bump` and
    `the_version_s_rules_compose_no_events`. `stellar/summaries`, `stellar/briefs`, the observed
    summaries and every other golden are unmoved: none of their stars is young or on the thermally
    pulsing AGB, and none writes events. The slow suites were not run so.
- **`M_V` follows the event light (research-leans-12 §3, 2026-10-31; amends the lane's lean on the
  wire).** `EventEffects::absolute_magnitude_v` is the track's `M_V` − 2.5 log₁₀(L ÷ L(t)) at the
  track's bolometric correction: exact in a thermal pulse, first-order for a flare's, an outburst's
  or an eruption's light. From 26 the wire's `absolute_v_mag` is it; `teff_k`, B − V, the summary's
  own `M_V` and `brief_at` stay the track's, and a wire field of the event light's own stays
  deferred. Movers at 26: none new. With both holds on, `scene_systems` moves only by an empty
  `active_events` on its 10 stars, none of which has an event running at its times.
