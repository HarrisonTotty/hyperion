# P06.T43.b: records as built

The as-built records of P06.T43.b, moved from the Risks and open points of
[06-stellar-stage.md](../../06-stellar-stage.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **P06.T43.b's swap as built, held for 26 (lane `p06-t43b`, 2026-10-31, at version 24; nothing
  moved).** Ask A4's photometry behind a stellar hold, not on the record.
  - _The hold._ `photometry::{V_MAGNITUDE_VERSION, VMagnitudeRules::{Interim, A4}}`, the field
    `StellarRules::v_magnitude`, chosen in one `match` in `absolute_magnitude_v_under`.
    `absolute_v_of_state_under` sends white dwarfs through it, and the disc's white dwarfs take it
    before their dwarf-table stand-in.
  - _Who reads the giants' correction (lean, 2026-10-31):_ every living phase past the hydrogen
    main sequence (Hertzsprung gap to post-AGB, the helium stars included), at its own log g,
    through `giant_bolometric_correction_v_under` (the rules' dwarf sequence from 5,000 K). Never
    a protostar, pre-main-sequence, main-sequence or substellar star; from 6,000 K it is the dwarf
    table's, bit for bit.
  - _The census under the arm (lean, 2026-10-31):_ it reads the galaxy's rules, as for the dwarf
    sequence. `StarBounds` restates the bounds that kept white dwarfs dark: a star that may be one
    (any pair-changed star, or one whose oldest age reaches `FINE_START` of its table lifetime)
    takes the brighter of its bound and `WHITE_DWARF_BRIGHTEST_V`, 4.0 (measured 4.639). The
    widened envelope is unchanged; the census test holds it to every white dwarf.
  - _Kept on the version's rules, refit at the bump:_ `sky_envelope`, `sky_phase_envelope`,
    `sky_binary_light_c/d/e`, `binary_pair_light_c/d/e` (and the pair light's held floor), and
    the sky's luminosity tables. `binary_cooling_floor` reads no star A4 moves.
  - Every white dwarf reads the hydrogen grid, whatever its `wd_spectral` atmosphere
    (research-p06 §3): helium atmospheres would differ by up to 0.24 mag from 5,000 to 40,000 K,
    and 0.4 above. A later task.
  - _For the bump to 26:_ the tripwire `the_a4_magnitudes_are_held_only_until_the_26_bump` lists
    the deletions and refits; the lane's outbox lists the movers.
