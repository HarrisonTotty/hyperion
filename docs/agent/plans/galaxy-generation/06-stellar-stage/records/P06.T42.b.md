# P06.T42.b: records as built

The as-built records of P06.T42.b, moved from the Risks and open points of
[06-stellar-stage.md](../../06-stellar-stage.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **P06.T42.b's swap as built, held for 26 (lane `a2-p06-t42b`, 2026-10-31, at version 24;
  nothing moved).** The rate is `Modern`'s behind a stellar hold, not on the record.
  - _The hold._ `wind::{HD_LIMIT_VERSION, HdLimitRules::{Belczynski, Enforced}}` and the field
    `StellarRules::hd_limit`. `wind::rate` takes the rules: its `Modern` arm is `modern` under
    `Belczynski` and `modern_limited` under `Enforced`. Its callers pass their `TrackOptions`'
    rules, except the engine's `main_sequence_structure`, which keeps the version's (the limit
    leaves main-sequence and helium winds alone). Two more readers act under `Enforced`. The
    eruptions are thinned by `is_luminous_blue_variable` at each candidate (research-p06 §8; the
    bound and so the bins' words are unchanged). The brief refuses the fate table and certifies no
    pair (`HdLimitRules::fitted_tables_hold`).
  - _Kept on the version's rules until the bump refits them:_ `stellar_fates_*` and through them
    `fates_for`, `GalaxyParams` and the potential (so the freeze holds under the hold), and the
    stripping, reach, pair-light, `kick_rank` fingerprint and sky tables.
    `stellar/rules/hd_limit_hold.rs` lists these and the other version-rules readers.
  - _The three checks_ (slow tests in `hd_limit_hold.rs`):
    1. Remnants: solar black holes from 50–150 M☉ fall from 10.4–20.2 to 10.0–12.2 M☉. A
       pair-unstable 150 M☉ star at [Fe/H] −2 instead collapses to the 40.5 M☉ cap.
    2. `envelope_knots`: every track of 25–150 M☉ at the envelope's 12 [Fe/H] nodes and three η
       builds, with at most 17 knots a segment, the same as under the constant.
    3. R06's envelope: the enforced tracks are up to 3.0 mag fainter in V at 150 M☉, and the
       committed table still bounds every bin. So the caps stay conservative until the refit.
  - _Research-p06 §8:_ under `Enforced` the LBV windows' ejecta are at most 1.29 times the
    track's loss over the window, and mostly under 0.05. The rate is not retuned.
    `lbv_eruptions_galaxy_wide_under_the_enforced_limit` gives 48.8 LBVs and 7.9 eruptions in
    ±H, below both windows (findings).
  - (lean, 2026-10-31) The ejecta stay untied to the track's Ṁ (design note 14). Retuning the
    rate to 30 eruptions in ±H (the plan's 15–60) would put the 150 M☉ windows' ejecta near five
    times the track's loss, so the 26 train settles the rate and the mass law together.
  - _For the bump to 26:_ the tripwire `the_hd_limit_is_held_only_until_the_26_bump` lists the
    deletions; the lane's outbox lists the movers.
  - (lean, 2026-10-31) Where the table route closes, the brief takes its exact routes rather than
    gaining an arm-keyed fate table: no fitted table is built under a non-version arm.
