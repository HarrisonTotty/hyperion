- **P06.T42.b's swap as built, held for 26 (lane `a2-p06-t42b`, 2026-10-31, at version 24;
  nothing moved).** The rate is `Modern`'s behind a stellar hold, not on the record.
  - _The hold._ `wind::{HD_LIMIT_VERSION, HdLimitRules}`, the field `StellarRules::hd_limit`.
    `rate`'s `Modern` arm is `modern_limited` under `Enforced`, through each track's options;
    `main_sequence_structure` keeps the version's rules, since no main-sequence or helium wind moves.
  - _Under `Enforced`:_ the eruptions are thinned by `is_luminous_blue_variable` at each
    candidate, the bound unchanged. The brief takes no table route and certifies no pair
    (`HdLimitRules::fitted_tables_hold`).
  - _Kept on the version's rules until 26:_ `stellar_fates_*` (so `fates_for`, `GalaxyParams`, the
    potential and the birth populations), stripping, reach, pair light, `kick_rank`'s fingerprint
    and the sky tables. `stellar/rules/hd_limit_hold.rs` lists the readers.
  - _Deviation: a hold, not a lane branch._ Held at 26 on integration (decision-stellar-holds
    §1.2). `kick_rank`'s fingerprint moves at 26, not 25, so the 26 train re-runs P06.T19.e's
    manifest for A2.
  - _The three checks_ (slow tests in `hd_limit_hold.rs`):
    1. Remnants: solar black holes from 50–150 M☉ fall from 10.4–20.2 to 10.0–12.2 M☉. A 150 M☉
       star at [Fe/H] −2 leaves none under the constant and the 40.5 M☉ pulsational cap under the
       limit.
    2. `envelope_knots`: tracks of 25–150 M☉ at the envelope's 12 [Fe/H] nodes and three η take
       at most 17 knots a segment, as under the constant.
    3. R06's envelope: up to 3.0 mag fainter in V at 150 M☉, the brightest node −9.80 (R06's
       ≈ −9.7), and the committed table bounds every bin. _Deviation:_ the caps read that table,
       so they are not re-measured until the 26 refit.
  - _Research-p06 §8:_ the ejecta are at most 1.29 times the track's loss (150 M☉, [Fe/H] 0),
    mostly under 0.05. The census gives 48.8 LBVs and 7.9 eruptions in ±H (181 and 30.2 under the
    constant), below both of the plan's windows (findings).
  - (lean, 2026-10-31) The ejecta stay untied to the track's Ṁ (design note 14). Retuning the
    rate to 30 eruptions in ±H would put the 150 M☉ windows' ejecta near five times the track's
    loss, so the 26 train settles the rate and the mass law together.
  - (lean, 2026-10-31) Where the table route closes, the brief takes its exact routes rather than
    gaining an arm-keyed fate table: no fitted table is built under a non-version arm.
  - _For the bump to 26:_ the tripwire `the_hd_limit_is_held_only_until_the_26_bump` lists the
    deletions, rewrites, refits and census re-runs; the lane's outbox lists the movers.
