# R10.T16.h: records as built

The as-built records of R10.T16.h, in the form of the plan's other record files
([10-terrain-on-generated-worlds.md](../../10-terrain-on-generated-worlds.md)). A new record goes at
the end, in the same form.

- **Deviations in T16.h, as built** (2026-11-01; lane seamless-no-collapse, the owner's priority;
  research-terrain-seamless's brief 1).
  - _The cache._ `PatchCache.insert` answers `refused` rather than evict a patch the last retained
    selection pins (selected, a resident ancestor of one, drawn, demanded); `pressure().exceeded`
    reports it, and `refusals` counts them. A forced arrival still takes the least recently used
    pinned patch, as before (lean, 2026-11-01: the contact's ground outranks the view's). The pass's
    observer gains `onRefused`.
  - _Sub-patches._ `DrawnPatch.within`: a missing selected patch drawn on its nearest resident
    ancestor's surface over its own area alone; `DrawSet.slots` may name a slot once a part. The
    ancestor stands in whole as before only past `MAX_SUB_PATCH_LEVELS` (13) levels, or where the
    selection outnumbers the slots. The instance record grows to 48 B by `sub : vec4u`, its `x`
    `subPatchWord(levels, i, j)`; `y`, `z` and `w` are 0, free for seamless-2's fades. R07's smooth
    figure's `PatchInstance` steps over it.
  - _Holds._ `CoarseFineHolds.update(keys, levels)`: each drawn area with the level it is drawn at,
    a sub-patch's its ancestor's; with no `levels`, as before, bit for bit.
  - _The margin._ `#withMargins` takes the last nearest ground a selection gave the view (carried
    past an empty one), floored at the camera's height above the drawn ground beneath it (resident
    heights): both 2-cycles at the survey's edge fed the margin's ground back into the next
    selection. The brief's lean, the carry alone, left the second (low, 252.55 s).
  - _The probe's bounds._ A collapse is the drawn count halving while the selection keeps half of
    its own (`isCollapse`); the bare halving (`drawnHalvings`) also counts the survey leaving the
    view (low, 253.55 s), and a selection that halves or doubles and comes back is a flicker. The
    coarsen bound is on each coarsen's level part, `SeamEvent.levelPx` (the finer side's distance
    from its own morph target); the whole move, `largestCoarsenPx`, is recorded: at the delta a
    rebake lands on one level before the other (16.8 px low, 27.4 px high), seamless-2's rebake
    fade.
  - _Suites changed with the rule._ `cache.test.ts`: the drawn patch evicted beside forced ones is
    refused, the draw set's stand-ins are parts (whole only by the two fallbacks), the T14.a sizes
    record's exceeded frames (low 242 → 702, wireframe 4 → 149: each refusal is reported).
    `terrainPassShared.test.ts`' seam checker reads areas and drawn levels.
    `fixedStep.ts`' coarse stand-ins measure the areas a stand-in draws. `discReflectance.test.ts`
    re-pins the plain kernels' digests.
  - _Not run:_ the owner's RTX views check, hidden, at 231–233 s (low) and 253–255 s (high), before
    and after (`just views-check --hidden --terrain --setting low|high`): pending: the owner's RTX
    run. The frame time with sub-patch instances: pending: the owner's RTX run.
