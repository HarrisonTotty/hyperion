  - _The shading normal's morph_ (§16's fix, built whatever the trace showed). `shadingNormalAt`
    blends the patch's own tile's normal to its parent's by k, normalize(mix(own, parent's, k)),
    the parent's tile read at the patch's place in it ((q + uv) ÷ 2) and only where k > 0, as
    `horizon.wgsl` blends the horizon; R07's horizon term, its φ and R11's decoration hook take that
    normal. The parent is the horizon blend's (`#parentSlotOf`): a parent resident and holding a
    map. On the lit layout every bake holds its normals and its map, so the one slot serves both
    blends; a parent holding no map blends neither (none arises on the lit style). R05's spike
    shading (`terrain.wgsl`) keeps its own tile: its records name no parent. At k = 1 a fine
    patch's normal along a coarse–fine edge equals its coarse neighbour's to about 10⁻¹⁶ rad on the
    test planet's real bakes (both read the same samples of the parent's level, bit for bit), on
    both settings' normals (65 and 129 samples a side), against 4.3° to 4.9° at k = 0
    (`shadingNormal.wasm.test.ts`, its east and north edges at level 12 over 11).
  - _The part-6 check_ (in `smoke/litTerrain.ts`'s group, "R10.T8.c a fine patch at k = 1 shades as
    its parents, normals and horizon"): the 3 × 3 block drawn at k = 1 must draw its four
    parents' own image, drawn alone at k = 0, to 1% at 98% of the texels both draw, and the block at
    k = 1 with no parent to blend to must not (so that the check can fail). As run on SwiftShader,
    both vertex paths and both variants: 99.82% of 1,655 texels within 1%; with no parent's tiles
    and maps 66.34% (high) and 68.04% (low).

  - _The multi-view morph invariant_ (§9: the test first, then the remedy it chose). The test
    (`terrainPassShared.test.ts`, "the morph at every coarse–fine edge in each view of a shared
    pass") checks, in each view of a settled shared pass, k = 1 at the fine side's 65 edge vertices
    and k = 0 at the coarse side's vertices of every coarse–fine edge and corner contact drawn
    under that view's mask (its band from the written record, its holds from the record's word),
    and equal k at every vertex two drawn patches of one level share. The study's pose (both views
    2,000 km straight over the pole) draws no coarse–fine edge, so its cases are 20 km over the pole
    tilted 0.5 rad, 640 × 360. Before the remedy:
    - an instrument at the primary's pose a quarter as wide: in the instrument, every one of its
      18 edges and 14 corner contacts with the coarse side at k = 1 (the primary none);
    - an instrument 80 km up on the same line of sight: 36 of 60 edges and 28 of 46 corners, the
      coarse side at k 0.842 to 1; with the views swapped, the primary breaks the same way;
    - **the primary alone breaks too**, which the study took as unproven: 1,000 km straight over
      the pole, 8 of its 8 edges, the coarse side at k 0.065 to 0.922 (the selection's
      8-neighbour balance splits rings no view's τ asked for, carrying coarse–fine edges past the
      coarse side's band start), and many tilted poses between 2,000 and 20 km;
    - a stand-in beside resident patches, the primary alone (the trace's case): 4 edges and 2
      corners, the fine side at k 0.233 to 0.359.
  - _The remedy._ The fine side reached 1 in every case but the stand-in's, so the bands from the
    levels each view draws (remedy a) could not serve: a per-level band exists only where the drawn
    levels lie in shells about the camera, and bands changing at each selection would pop. Built:
    remedy (b), the morph held at coarse–fine edges by the selection, as **per-edge holds in both
    directions** over the draw set as resolved, stand-ins included, once a selection and the same
    for every view (`coarseFineHolds.ts`): a vertex of a drawn patch on an edge or corner that a
    finer drawn patch touches is held at k = 0, one that a coarser drawn patch touches at k = 1.
    Whole edge rows and single corners, no ramp (a ramp would part two same-level patches at the
    perpendicular edges' corners); what remains is a one-row fold of about τ pixels. 16 bits a patch
    (4 edges and 4 corners at k = 0, the same at k = 1) in the record's quadrant word, bits 2 to 17;
    the record stays 32 B, and every reader of the quadrant masks it. After it every case's every
    edge and corner holds, the primary-alone control and F1's pin (the primary's records equal the
    single view's bit for bit) unchanged; and the tests keep, as their control, that the bands alone
    break the instrument's, the 1,000 km and the stand-in's cases. Cost: 3.2 ms a selection for 2,560 patches in a worst-case
    layout, under vitest at a load of 21 (provisional); nothing a frame.
    - _Deviations._ (1) Both sides are held, not the coarse side alone: the stand-ins' fine side
      needs it. (2) A vertex under both holds (neighbours two levels apart, only beside a stand-in)
      takes k = 1, the fine side's step being the one seen. (3) A neighbour two or more levels
      away is held as one a level away: the step is smaller, not closed, under `STREAMING`.
      (4) R05's Design note 6 takes k as a function of the vertex's position alone; with the
      holds it is one of the position and of the draw set, the same in every view, which keeps its
      crack-freedom (every patch sharing a vertex holds it alike). (5) The hold is applied inside
      `morphFactor`, before the grounded contacts' minimum, rather
      than after it: a k = 1 hold after it would override the grounded hold under a craft; the
      forced region's patches meet coarser ones only past every contact's ramp, so nothing else
      moves. The depth-only and wireframe materials and R05's spike compose the same
      `patchVertexOf`, so they follow; R07's smooth figure has its own records and no holds
      (deferred, below).

- **Deferred findings (2026-10-13 rule) (R10.T8.c)** (recorded, not ruled; each leaves the

  - _High's streaming never catches up in the north look_ (for R10.T14.b, main's ruling): three
    height workers, two bakes each in flight, about 9 to 11 bakes a second, against some 640
    patches selected and a turnover of tens a second at the script's 1.8 to 2.3 km/s ground speed

    baked patches beneath it (`cache.ts`), a refused bake waits forever once no block will come.
  - _R07's smooth figure has no holds_ (`smoothMesh.ts`, `smoothMesh.wgsl`): its single-view
    coarse–fine edges can morph the coarse side as the terrain's did 1,000 km over the pole.
  - _`effectiveTauPx`'s doc and its budget test speak of the fine side alone_; the coarse side is
    the holds', tested in `terrainPassShared.test.ts`; and the bands' margin argument does not
    cover the balance's ring splits (moot for the seams).
  - _A stand-in two levels coarser_ keeps a smaller step and the fine side's held row folds by
    more than τ pixels there: a stand-in's, under `STREAMING`.
  - _The margin's residual in the last degrees of sun._ A vertex two patches share sees, looking out,
    8 vertices of its neighbour and the far field's lerp beyond, and looking in its whole patch:
    up to 2.3° apart on the Moon-like rim (a narrow ridge past the margin can give more), which a
    sun under a degree or two shows as a faint line along an edge (236 s on high, 232 s on low).
    Levers: M = 16 (halves it; `OWN_BAND` and the memo double, a cold bake some 25–35 ms more), or
    a window of a whole neighbour patch (exact, about 34,000 heights a bake).
  - _The memo's key at the finest levels_ (since T8.a; the margin is a second reader). `memo_key`
    packs i and j in 28 bits each, but the patch's own level's indices need 29 from level 22
    (`MAX_LEVEL` 24), where keys collide and a bake would read another vertex's height; patches
    stop near level 19–20, so nothing reaches it. Lever: assert the level, or widen the key.
  - _The memo fills sooner_ by the margin's 2,336 heights a cold bake (T8.a's "The far memo starts
    afresh wholesale").
