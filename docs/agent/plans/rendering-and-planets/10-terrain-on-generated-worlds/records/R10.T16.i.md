# R10.T16.i: records as built

The as-built records of R10.T16.i, in the form of the plan's other record files
([10-terrain-on-generated-worlds.md](../../10-terrain-on-generated-worlds.md)). A new record goes at
the end, in the same form.

- **Deviations in T16.i, as built** (2026-11-02; lane seamless-time-morph, the owner's priority;
  research-terrain-seamless's brief 2).
  - _The fades' fields._ The record stays 48 B: `sub.y` the edges' fades, `sub.z` the corners'
    (bytes), `sub.w` `fadeWord(own, mix, oldSlot)`: the own fade's byte, the heights mix's fade in
    4095 steps (`MIX_STEPS`, `mixStep`) and its older slot in 12 bits, `DATUM_SLOT` (0xfff) for a
    rise, `PARENT_MIX_SLOT` (0xffe) for a refine's or a coarsen's parent; a rebake whose spare slot
    reaches them swaps at once. `coarseFineHolds.ts`' `fadeFloor`, `fadedMorph` and
    `TransitionFades` twin `terrainPatch.wgsl`. Bit 18 of `parentQuadrant` is untouched.
  - _For R14's cull._ A fading instance names its newer slot in `slot` and its older in `sub.w`'s
    top 12 bits (its `parentSlot` for `PARENT_MIX_SLOT`); a rise, and a mix over ground the older
    surface held unsurveyed, go towards h = 0, so the union takes in the datum. A fade lasts up to
    `MORPH_MAX_MS` (16 s).
  - _The morph._ `timeMorph.ts`' `TimeMorph`, called by the pass's `#present` and `#fadesAt`; a
    change starts at its first draw (`TerrainViewFrameInput.nowMs`) and advances at each draw over
    the span its size on screen sets then (`MorphSpans`, the pass's `#screenPx`): at least
    `MORPH_IN_MS` (600 ms; `morphInMs` on the pass and session), 37.5 ms a pixel
    (`MORPH_MS_PER_PX`), at most 16 s. So the fades depend on the views' cameras too, one value a
    transition still.
  - _The parent mix._ A refine's and a coarsen's heights also move by the parent's own surface at
    the instance's place (`parentPlaceOf`; the datum where the parent's survey held it) less their
    morph target, by the fade, so that bakes that disagree (the survey's delta, its edge) fade;
    their span counts the disagreement (`parentDisagreementM`). Where the bakes agree it moves
    nothing, and shared vertices agree.
  - _A level at a time_ (lean, 2026-11-01). A refine of more than one level draws the resident
    ancestors between first, a span each, and a patch still falling holds a finer area until it
    ends. A coarsen of more than one level raises the finest patches to their parents' mesh alone,
    and its swap shows the levels between.
  - _Rebakes and rises._ `PatchCache.replace` takes a spare slot and retires the older one until
    its fade ends (`releaseRetired`; `usedSlots` and `heldBytes` count it); with none spare, swap at
    once, counted (`rebakesSwapped`); a patch leaving the cache ends its fade (`TimeMorph.forget`).
    A patch drawn where nothing was, over ground a view saw at the last step, rises from the datum.
  - _The budget's hold_ (lean, 2026-11-02). `SelectionInput.budgetHold` (`splitKeysOf` the last
    selection) refuses a new split past `1 − margin` of `maxPatches`, so `limitExcess` and the bands'
    τ′ start there; `budgetHoldMargin` (pass, session), `BUDGET_HOLD_MARGIN` (15%) by default, `null`
    for none, as before bit for bit.
  - _The hand-over's first draw._ A pass draws no ground until every seen selected patch has a
    resident patch to draw it (`Device.firstDrawn`).
  - _The bands' slew._ `slewedTauPx` (`BAND_SLEW_PER_STEP`, 2% a step); selection's τ and
    `limitExcess` are not slewed.
  - _Reduced motion_ (the UX review). `useTerrainSession`'s `reducedMotion`, from VIEW's
    `usePrefersReducedMotion`, sets `TerrainSession.setReducedMotion` and its passes': every change
    then ends at its first draw (`footprintsAt`'s `cut`); the distance's morph stays.
  - _The wireframe's pass_ (lean, 2026-11-02; the UX review, principle 5): no rise, no parent mix,
    and a rebake replaced in its slot at once, so that its contours state one bake's elevations.
  - _Not faded_ (lean, 2026-11-01): a draft's refined normals (rewritten in place); a parent's
    rebake in its children's normals at k > 0 and in their parent mix (`parentSlot` moves at once);
    and the survey mask's edge where a finer bake's mask leaves ground its parent drew. Two
    neighbouring rebakes of different spans disagree on their shared edge mid-fade, under the skirts.
  - _The probe._ `SeamRun.frameChanges`, `pops` and `flipFlops`; `SeamSummary`'s `frame*`,
    `appear*` and `flipFlops*`; it reads the frame before's ground by this frame's camera and bands.
    Knobs `SEAM_MORPH_MS`, `SEAM_BUDGET_HOLD`, `SEAM_FRAME_STRIDE` (8: every eighth grid line of a
    changed instance). The slow tests pin the figures as measured at the default knobs and play the
    hand-over with drafts at half a bake's cost.
  - _Not run:_ the RTX 3080's frame time with the fade term, TERRAIN TEST high, against the
    15.67 ms p95 (`just views-check --hidden --terrain --setting high`): pending: the owner's RTX
    run.
- **The flip-flops at 8 workers, as built** (2026-11-02; lane seamless-time-morph, main's item
  after R14.T6.a).
  - _The frustum margin's hold_ (lean, 2026-11-02). With a step's clock, a view's widest frustum
    margin is held 1.5 s (`FRUSTUM_MARGIN_HOLD_MS`, `heldFrustumMargin`); with none, the rule's.
    The 0.44 a second (high, 8 workers, drafts at half) were all at the survey's exit, 250–253 s:
    one step's near ground opened the margin fourfold for three selections, and the ground it
    opened onto took the budget's margin and coarsened again at once.
  - _The budget's hold stays 15%._ Tried and dropped: held splits' priority raised 25% (34–80
    flip-flops a second), a margin's refusal expiring after 2 s (11–21, the tail to 150 s), and
    30% (none, tail 43 s).
  - _The 56 s tail is withdrawn demand._ On high at 3 workers the hold splits 1/8/5/229 at 88.25 s,
    1 s after no hold does. Its children's ground leaves the selection 0.1–0.4 s later, and they
    are demanded again at 141–145 s. `demandSpanToResidentS` times the demand's last unbroken span:
    18 s at most.
  - _Measured_ at the tip, 0–300 s: high 3×41: 0 flip-flops a second, demand → resident max 56 s
    (span 18 s). Low 3×41: 0, 3.9 s. High 8×41, drafts at half: 0, 1.2 s. Low: 0.05 (0.03 before),
    0.7 s. Holes, collapses, jumps and evictions are 0 in all four, and pops are unchanged.
  - _Low's 0.05 a second._ Its twelve events are at the survey's delta (120 s) and exit
    (249–253 s), each where a parent's rebake had landed a frame before.
- **The Mac A/B's items, as built** (2026-11-03; lane seamless-time-morph).
  - _Patches over the budget._ Selection and the resolver stay within `maxPatches`; the draw set
    passes it by a coarsen's held finer patches (652 at most against 604 on the probe on high, in
    1,041 of 15,565 frames; 881 on the owner's Mac). Ending holds at the budget, in the set's order
    or the furthest along first, moved the ground 26 px in a frame on high and 17 px on low, against
    the probe's 6: dropped (lean, 2026-11-03). The check judges the selection instead (R10.T16.d).
  - _Stand-ins on the cluster path._ Every patch of an interior bake draws on the cluster path, a
    sub-patch kept whole by the cull (`ViewState.interiorWhole`), in every pass. On SwiftShader a
    transition frame of the smoke's fixture (31 stand-ins of 65) took 1,208 ms against 1,594 ms,
    its patches 46 on the cluster path and 19 on the edge path against 18 and 46; settled 872 and
    890 ms.
  - _Not built:_ Design note 19's mask, a stand-in drawn as its ancestor's own blocks at its level
    offset, which needs the cull or the cluster vertex stage (other lanes'); its gain is the rest.
