# R10.T14.f: records as built

The as-built records of R10.T14.f, moved from the Risks and open points of
[10-terrain-on-generated-worlds.md](../../10-terrain-on-generated-worlds.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **Deviations in T14.f, as built** (2026-10-21; lane r10-point-stall, on `rendering-and-planets`
  at `8786bdc0`, fast-forwarded to `85d3e054`; decision-next-lane-2026-10-21b §1–§2).
  - _Files._ The entry's: `view/bodies/classMapPoint.ts` and its test; `view/bodies/draw.ts`'s
    point path alone (`pointFlux` names each light to `pointReflectionOf`, a doc comment);
    `view/terrain/classMap.ts` and its test;
    `view/terrain/session.ts`, whose step runs the slice first, and its test. Nothing in
    `view/atmosphere/`, `smoke/`, `lib/system/` or the shaders.
  - _The lever: slices on the render thread, not a worker._ The build is the old loops as
    generators, yielding every 16 elements' shares or 16 normals, a row of the geometry, or a block
    copy, so that the sliced and the synchronous paths run the same code in the same order and the
    ratio is the same bit for bit by construction; a worker would have needed the reconstructions
    beside it, the 3.1 MB geometry sent back, and a protocol and lifetime of its own. The slices run
    in `TerrainSession.step` (`stepClassMapPoints`, once a primary frame, at most 2 ms and a step,
    `CLASS_MAP_POINT_SLICE_MS`); one module-wide queue, the oldest first, as the module's caches
    are module-wide. The store queues a map's build as it takes it, before dropping the old map,
    and cancels it as it drops the map or releases the body; the frame's path queues any surface it
    finds unqueued (tests' and harnesses' surfaces). `classMapReflectionRatio` stays the
    synchronous path, and `makeClassMapPointNow` makes a surface's elements at once, for tests and
    harnesses; `classMapPointState` and `classMapPointWork` report the queues.
  - _The stand-ins._ Until a map's elements are made its point is drawn by the replaced map's last
    handed-out surface where that one's elements were made, else by that map's own stand-in, else
    the uniform point of `elsewhere`, bit for bit what the body drew before a map came. The store
    keeps the handed-out surface across uploads (`HeldMap.handedOut`), since a device restore
    forgets the one it hands out. Beyond the entry: as a map's elements are made, what its point
    was drawn with (the stand-in's held ratios, or a ratio of 1 where it was drawn uniform) is kept
    as the map's provisional ratios, which stand in past δ, as a held ratio does (below), until the
    map's own are found in the slices, and are let go when its last own refresh lands; so the frame
    on which the elements are made evaluates no light. They go at the next slice if no frame drew
    the point meanwhile (a map made while its body was a disc then meets the disc at its first
    frame as a point), and pass to a map that replaces it before its own are found. A build that
    throws is said once (a failed map is never queued again) and its point stays its stand-in's.
  - _The shares stored in blocks of 65,536 entries_ and copied into their stores a block a step:
    the map's entries copied at once (614,400 on the bench's eight-class map) made the longest
    slices in a loaded provisional run, up to 27 ms.
  - _The misses, sliced too_ (acceptance 5). A light's evaluation on a cache miss at level 8,
    quiet: 5.62 ms at 0°, 3.94 ms at 60° and 2.33 ms at 120° on the synthetic Iapetus, 6.37, 4.42
    and 2.56 ms on an eight-class map, over the 4 ms line; under time acceleration a fast rotator
    missed every frame past δ, four lights 22–25 ms a frame at phase 0. So past δ the light's own
    nearest held ratio within `classMapPointStaleAngleRad`, 0.25 rad × (π − α) ÷ π, stands in and
    the fresh one is found in the same slices (`ratioSteps`, 256 elements a step, the synchronous
    sums' order), held in place of that entry (`holdRefreshed`), so that a refresh never lets
    another light's only entry go from the eight a surface holds. The held ratios carry their
    light's key (`classMapPointReflection`'s new `lightKey`, from `pointFlux`: `star <index>` for a
    star, the neighbour's body for planetshine), so that two lights within the stale angle of each
    other (a circumbinary planet's stars, a moon's star and planet near conjunction) never stand in
    for each other; within δ any light's ratio is taken, as before. A light's first evaluation, with
    nothing of its own held so near, is still found on the frame: the body's first frame as a point
    under a map made before, which must meet the disc it switches with (R07's 1%). The
    evaluation's loop is a plain function over a range of elements (`addLawSums`): as a generator
    it was 0.2–0.6 ms slower a miss. _After R10.T1.c's client half: under a map's maturity an
    evaluation is about a third longer (0.99 to 1.30 ms a light, provisional, load 1.08)._
  - _T10.f's cache bound past δ._ Between δ and the stale angle the drawn ratio is the held one
    while the fresh one is found, a refresh at level 8 taking about three slices a light: under fast
    time acceleration the point's light curve lags the turn by those frames, by up to 0.33 of the
    ratio on the Iapetus at 0.01 rad a frame (below). Within δ, T10.f's 1.7 × 10⁻³ stands. The test
    "finds the ratio afresh once the light moves past δ" became the held ratio past δ and the
    refreshed one bit for bit once found, and the fresh one at once past the stale angle.
  - _Bit for bit._ The finished ratio after the slices equals the synchronous path's at the three
    views and three phases on level-6, 7 and 8 checkerboards (the committed test); and, in a scratch
    check against 8786bdc0's module, both paths equal the base's frame and synchronous ratios at 448
    geometries (two maps, one of eight classes summing past 1; no normals and levels 6, 7 and 8; a
    sphere and a spheroid; four views; seven phases).

    thread's longest stop for this work falls from 1.3–1.7 s to about 2.2 ms, under R05's hitch row

  - _The frames under time acceleration_ (provisional, load 6 to 9, after the misses' fixes; four
    lights, level 8, one view, the body turned about its pole each frame): at 0.002 rad a frame the
    frame's path takes a median 0.02 ms and a p99 of 0.08 ms (Iapetus) or 2.4 ms (eight classes);
    at 0.01 rad a median 0.02 ms and a p99 of 6.2–8.5 ms, a light now and then past the stale angle
    at high phase; at 0.03 rad a median 2.2–2.4 ms and a p99 of 13–15 ms. The slice's median is
    2.01 ms. The drawn ratio's largest lag error, sampled every 20 frames: 3.6% (0.002), 33%
    (0.01) and 24% (0.03) on the Iapetus; at most 7 × 10⁻⁴ on the eight-class map.
  - _The stand-in's step at readiness_ (acceptance 4): the Iapetus's first map against the uniform
    point it replaces, at most |ratio − 1| = 1.77 over the three views at 0°, 60° and 120°, the map
    itself arriving as before, now when its elements are made and not when it is taken; a remade
    level-8 map (half the Iapetus surveyed, the survey's edge moved by 0.02 in its plane's offset)
    against the map before it, at most 2.4%.
  - _The remake_ (acceptance 6), checked: each block that surveys cells brings a new map, its texels
    and normals new objects (the store's test), so its elements are made again. Before T14.f each
    remade map stopped the render thread again, 0.3–1.7 s, wherever a view drew the body as a
    point; now each remake queues a build in slices and cancels the older's unfinished one, and the
    point stays on the last made map until the new one is made. No stall. Surveys come only from
    `tools/placeShip.ts` today (no console sends a `survey_pass`), and a field's first load answers
    a map at each of its first blocks, one query out at a time.
  - _Memory_ (acceptance 7), recorded, not counted under a budget row (render-thread CPU memory,
    outside R12's GPU categories): the elements' geometry 3.1 MB once (2.36 MB of directions, 0.79
    MB of solid angles); a map's shares 0.39 MB of offsets and 9 B an entry (1.3 MB for the
    Iapetus's 99,488 entries, 5.9 MB for the eight-class map's 614,400); its normals, `f32`, 1.2 MB
    at levels 5 and 6, 4.7 MB at 7 and 18.9 MB at 8. While a remake's build runs, the stand-in's
    elements stay beside the new build's, up to about twice a map's (some 50 MB at level 8 with
    eight classes). _After R10.T1.c's client half: under a maturity 21 B an entry, its three `f32`
    factors 12 B (7.4 MB more for the eight-class map's 614,400), and a level-8 build 637 → 715
    slices (+12%, provisional)._
  - _Not built._ The optional quarter-point path decoding each normal texel once (T10.g's lever),
    and T10.f's cheaper reconstruction loop and merged uniform texels: they would shorten the
    build, not the slices. The optional hidden RTX 3080 run reading T14.d's gap probe across the
    3 px switch: the quiet bench is the record.
- **Deferred findings (2026-10-13 rule) (R10.T14.f)** (recorded, not ruled; each leaves the
  algorithm and the contracts as they are).
  - _Frames to ready._ A level-8 map is made in 645–815 slices, 11–14 s at 60 Hz and twice that at
    a 30 Hz primary; a surveying run that remakes the map faster keeps the point on the last made
    map. The levers: the quarter-point path and a reconstruction loop cheaper than
    `classMapSharesAt`'s allocations (the build, not the slices), or a worker.
  - _2 ms a frame while surveying,_ whether or not any view draws the body as a point, since every
    map the store takes is queued (as ruled, so that its elements are usually made before the body
    is a point). The lever: queue only bodies within some multiple of the 3 px switch.
  - _The lag under fast time acceleration_: up to 0.33 of the ratio on the Iapetus at 0.01 rad a
    frame, the light curve about 12 frames late over four lights; past the stale angle a miss is
    found on the frame again (p99 6–9 ms at 0.01 rad a frame, 13–15 ms at 0.03).
  - _A light's first evaluation stays on the frame_: up to 6.4 ms a light at level 8, about 25 ms
    for four lights on the body's first frame as a point under a map made before, within R05's
    50 ms hitch row and over the entry's 4 ms a light. The lever: a provisional ratio from the
    disc's own light at the switch, or the evaluation begun in the slices while the body is a disc
    near the switch. _After R10.T1.c's client half: about a third longer under a map's maturity
    (T1.c's client-half deferred finding 12)._
  - _A remake's memory_: the stand-in's elements beside the new build's, up to about twice a map's.
  - _Slices past 2.2 ms_: one of 5.21 ms in the quiet run (the first level-7 build), a collection
    or a compile; provisional loaded runs before the block copy reached 27 ms.
  - _One queue for the module_: a second terrain session would step the slices twice a frame (one
    session in the app today); a surface the frame queued that no owner cancels stays queued until
    made (tests' and harnesses' surfaces only).
  - _The held ratios are a surface's_ (T10.f's, standing): after a device restore or a new
    `elsewhere` the store hands out a new surface over the same map, whose every light is found on
    the frame again (up to 6.4 ms a light at level 8). The lever: carry the held ratios over where
    the texels, normals and `elsewhere` are unchanged, or key them by those.
