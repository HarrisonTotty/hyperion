    the session through `useTerrainSession` over it; the fixture's revision is raised before the
    first ask, so the first answer is payload 3, the revision-2 survey. The main view is `VIEW`'s
    `ViewFrameDrawer` over the spike's kept scene (`SpikeSessionView`), stepped as `VIEW`'s primary:
    `terrainReportOf` → `noteView` (with `readoutBody` the moon) → `stepTerrainSession` under its

  - _The metrics on the terrain world._ Each frame's sample reads the pass in the style drawn
    (`session.pass` or `wirePass`, its `lastStep`); `streaming` is `TERRAIN: STREAMING` on the drawn
    frame. `onSelect` and `onPatch` come through `TerrainPass.observe`, re-observed when the
    session's pass changes: `requested` the first time a key enters the selection's demand, `baked`
    and `resident` both at the pass's `onResident` (the session's lane cannot be listened to, so a
    bake the cache refuses is not counted), and `onSelect` misses each pass's first selection, which
    the session makes in the step that makes the pass. `passesSubmitted` is `null` there, adding no
    untimed pass: neither the engine nor the drawer counts a frame's passes, and the metric's
    `max(0, n − 64)` takes a frame as one resolve where a drawer's frame spans many (about 15
    photorealistic). No `terrain.select` span is recorded, and the drawer's pass labels mostly fall
    in Design note 21's "other" row.
  - _The measurement_ (`bodyDescent.ts`; ruling §4). `holdBodyField` notes the measuring view, steps

    site, bakes through one lane of the session's pool (`TerrainSession.lane`, weights and horizon

    the spike's measuring effect aborts on unmount.

      cannot slip past a cell's edge. A candidate whose floors refuse the profile gives way to the
      next draw; the bakes are kept across attempts.

      `surveyed_reading`'s) in `ELEVATION`'s form, Missing until answered; the slant range from 1 km
      above is the query.
    - _The floors._ A stretch whose patches are all wholly surveyed takes its baked maximum plus ε_n

      the header's realised relief and a metre, `reading.rs`' `ceiling_m`), which bounds all ground
      at every level, so the floor there is the ceiling itself.
    - _σ_n._ The workers' table carries no σ_n, so `omittedSigmaM` is +∞ at every level: the
      calibrated count min(ε_n, 4σ_n) is the hard one. Nothing serialises it.

  - _Other craft at the present, bodies at their emission_ (decision-r10-t14c-leans, "Seen, not

    local one where it is seen, so a contact against a moon seen across light time τ stands off by

    today (the server's craft source is `NoCraft`; kept scenes have no light time). Owner: the plan

  - _A craft at rest over a coarse bake_ (2). Its read ground, perhaps a wholly surveyed bake's 17 ×
    17 grid two levels coarser, can lie more than r_g below it, and it is then no contact until the
    view's own selection refines under it; harmless in the picture (wherever the craft spans a pixel
    the drawn ground is within τ pixels). The lever: judge "grounded" on the read clearance less the
    read bake's stated bound. Owner: R11, once landed craft are seen from afar.
