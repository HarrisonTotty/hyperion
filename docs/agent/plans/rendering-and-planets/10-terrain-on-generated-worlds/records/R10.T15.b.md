  - _Item 1, the descent spike's served world._
    - _The launch._ `--world served --universe <ID> --body <ID>` (the client), both needed by
      `--world served` and taken by no other world; `SpikeLaunch.served` holds them, and the
      results file records them as the options `universe` and `body` and in the summary's
      `Launch:` line. `--style` and `--sizes on` as over `TERRAIN TEST`; `--ridged on`,
      `--vertex-path` and `--normals` refused as there. The client's watchdog gains 21 minutes (the
      field's 10-minute hold, one site's 10 minutes of bakes, a minute).
    - _The script._ `descentSpike.sh --world served [--universe <NAME|ID>] [--body <ID>]
[--survey <SOURCE> <M>] [--distance <AU>]` builds the server first, starts it as before,
      places the ship with `place-ship --json --survey orbital 50` (and the options given) once
      the server listens, and hands the client the placement's universe and body. Its outer limit
      gains 21 minutes.
    - _The world_ (`view/scene/servedBody.ts`, `servedSpikeWorld`): the body as the server's scene
      draws it at its first push, through `fromServer.ts` (`serverSceneAtPush`; its section's
      rotation law exported as `bodyRotationAt`): its designation, kind (planet, dwarf planet or
      moon), figure with its pole, Hill radius at pericentre, appearance and rotation. A contact,
      a giant's envelope, a body with no figure, Hill radius or rotation, a scene with no star or
      no stated place, is no served world, and the gap is named. The host discs are the server
      sky's (`servedHostDiscs`: at the body, at the push's time, `exclude_system` its system, a
      camera limit of V −30 that no star outside the system reaches, `n_max` 1).
    - _In the spike's kept scene_ the body stands at its own distance from a star of its own
      radius (`SpikeWorld.starDistanceM`, `starRadiusM`; 1 au and the Sun's nominal radius
      otherwise), turns by its rotation law at the push's time plus the script's seconds, and its
      scene's clock counts from that time (`SpikeWorld.epoch`), so that `TIME` is the body's. It
      carries no detail seed: the session asks `body_detail`, as `VIEW`'s does. The sun stands
      over the site's meridian at touchdown, as on the other worlds.
    - _The measurement_ is T14.c's, unchanged: the site among cells whose 3 × 3 block is
      surveyed, the track over surveyed ground, T14.c's floors. The field is held at revision 1
      (any survey), within 10 minutes (`SERVED_FIELD_HOLD_TIMEOUT_MS`), against `TERRAIN TEST`'s
      revision 2 within 2 minutes; a `not_modelled` field throws `FieldNotServed`, as the
      measurement's other refusals throw.
    - _The page_ (`ServedDescentSpike`): its own link (`useServerConnection` on
      `window.hyperion.serverUrl`), the scene subscribed for the run (its surface events relayed
      to the session), and while the world is read the spike's frame, its header now a component
      of its own (`SpikeHeader`) with the `MEASUREMENT` banner and, over a served world, the link's
      annunciator. Its line reads the link's state while the link is not up (`ESTABLISHING LINK`,

      reads in the guide's words with the remedy that fits it: `LINK INCOMPATIBLE`;
      `SCENE REJECTED: <reason>, relaunch to retry`; `SCENE TIMED OUT: relaunch to retry`;
      `TERRAIN NOT MEASURED: <body> is no served world (<gap>), relaunch with another body`;
      `TERRAIN NOT MEASURED: served world not read, relaunch to retry` (the sky's discs refused).
    - _The host star_ (lean, 2026-10-29): the first star up the body's parents, else the
      system's first star for a body that orbits a pair or the barycentre, since a world flown
