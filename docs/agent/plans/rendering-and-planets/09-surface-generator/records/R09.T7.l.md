# R09.T7.l: records as built

The as-built records of R09.T7.l, moved from the Risks and open points of
[09-surface-generator.md](../../09-surface-generator.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **Deviations in R09.T7.l, as built** (2026-10-29/30; the refit, held for the 25 train with T7.g's
  swap; `decision-r09-t7g-table` §1–§4).
  - _The cold nodes by index._ The manifest lists `cold_colatitude_indices` (21: every colatitude
    node to e₀ ÷ w 1, every second beyond) and `cold_limb_indices` (15: every limb node to D ÷ w
    0.4, every fourth beyond), in place of T7.g's `cold_colatitude_stride` and `cold_limb_stride`.
    The table carries them as `COLD_COLATITUDE_INDEX` and `COLD_LIMB_INDEX` (`u16`), and both reads
    take each cold node's shadow by them. `COLD` is 2,205 rows. On a stand-in of T7.g's values
    re-laid with strided indices, the reader's 307 pinned reads were unchanged to the bit.
  - _The reach_ is 512 px, the grid, and _16 surfaces_ are traced. The seed is T7.g's, so surfaces
    0–7 are T7.g's surfaces.
  - _The heating at the disk's centre, for one α_ (lean, 2026-10-29).
    - The shadow is tested under a point at the upper limb, D. The facets are lit by a point at the
      disk's centre on its path at max(0, D − α), and the fractions are over S sin(e₀ + max(0,
      D − α)), that point's own elevation, so `irradiance_limit` corrects nothing twice.
    - The table has no α axis: one α serves it, the manifest's `star_radius_deg` 0.2711°, carried
      as `STAR_RADIUS`. It is the Sun at the Moon's perihelion, 959.6″ at 1 au over 0.98329 au (the
      Earth–Moon barycentre's perihelion), as §8.13 takes it; the IAU 2015 B3 nominal radius gives
      959.23″ and 0.27098°, 0.04% less.
    - For the Moon the traced point is the reader's own `e_eff` at every latitude. For another α the
      normaliser corrects the heating to first order only, and beyond it the error runs one way. A
      body whose α is larger (Mercury, 0.87°) has its facets lit too high, its heating overstated
      and its cold traps understated: conservative. A far body (α 0.01–0.05°) has them lit
      0.22–0.26° too low and its cold traps overstated, most in the cold tail: perhaps 1.3–1.5
      times at δ 1.5° and more at δ ≲ 1° (the science check's estimate from the independent
      tracer's figures, not traced).
    - So supervolatile traps on bodies whose α is far from 0.2711° beside their δ wait for an α
      axis on the cold shares (at 0.01°, 0.2711° and 0.87°, say; the shadow needs none) or for
      direct traces at their α. QUESTIONS takes it to main.
  - _The task's revision_ is 1.
  - _The direct trace in the run._ The run also traces the real Moon at its own D at 80.25–89.75°
    every 0.5° on the same surfaces, at the four check slopes, each a node of its own at its own
    fractions. Its acceptance line gives the read against it, area-weighted: the acceptance's
    "direct trace at that D", on the same surfaces, so that the surfaces' spread does not enter.
  - _Public items beyond the text:_ `Params::star_radius` (`Radians`); `cold_colatitude_indices`
    and `cold_limb_indices`, in place of the strides; `Axes::cold_colatitude_indices` and
    `cold_limb_indices`; `direct_latitudes_deg`, `DirectCounts`, `DirectFigures`, `ReadAndTrace`,
    `RunCounts::direct` and `Figures::direct`; and the table's `COLD_COLATITUDE_INDEX`,
    `COLD_LIMB_INDEX` and `STAR_RADIUS`. `STAR_RADIUS` is in radians with no unit in its name (the
    Rust review's finding): it is generated, and renaming it moves the table's body, which the
    production run had already begun to write, so the rename waits for the table's next run.
  - _The golden_ `cold_trap/rough_plains` gains `irradiance_limit` and `fraction` (55 labels, from
    the determinism review of T7.g's normaliser), an extension on the stand-in.
  - _The spectrum check's pilot_ (`decision-r09-t7g-table` §3; scratch, never committed). Hayne et
    al.'s cited construction, |k|^−2(1+H) = |k|^−3.8 from 4 to 32 cycles on 128², 16 surfaces,
    scaled to the directional slope, rays to the grid, through the refit's own task (H 2.8 in its
    form, a scratch-only change to its refusal of H above 1) and axes. It took 3.3 h wall at 2
    threads beside other runs (pod, 8 vCPU).
    - Its figures: instantaneous shadow within 3.6% of Smith 1967's at 0.5–4°; over 80–90° at
      σ_s 5.7°, 11.3°, 16.7° and 26.6°, permanent shadow 2.78%, 11.87%, 20.10% and 31.98%, and
      under the real Sun water 1.99%, 3.66%, 2.35% and 1.16% and CO₂ 0.0209%, 0.0020%, 0.0003%
      and 0.0007%. Its read against its own direct trace at the Moon's D: shadow 0.0229 against
      0.0227 at σ_s 5.7°, water 0.0200 against 0.0208, CO₂ 0.000210 against 0.000235. These match
      the independent tracer's pilot of the same construction (Smith within 4%; CO₂ 0.019% at
      5.7°).
    - _The Moon's CO₂ bracket under it._ `the_moons_co2_cold_traps_lie_within_schorghofers_bracket`
      in an uncommitted copy, the craters by their bowls and the plains (σ_s 5.7°) through the
      reader on the pilot's table, half the Moon below 52.9 K and 55.6 K: with Hayne et al.'s
      distribution B, 14.7 km² (plains 13.0) and 37.3 km² (plains 30.0); with A, 13.0 km² and
      30.1 km². Against the bracket's 30–408 km², the cited construction fails at 52.9 K with
      both distributions and passes at 55.6 K by under a quarter. Today's bowls give 87.3 and
      198.5 km² (B), 85.6 and 191.2 km² (A).
    - The refit's own spectrum under the bracket is recorded with the production run.
