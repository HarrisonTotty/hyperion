# R09.T13.n: records as built

The as-built records of R09.T13.n, moved from the Risks and open points of
[09-surface-generator.md](../../09-surface-generator.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **Deviations in R09.T13.n, as built** (2026-10-30; lane r09-t13n; main's rulings on
  research-r09-t13m-landing's Part 2).
  - _New items._ In `partition`, `budget_in_air` (`budget` is now the tests' form),
    `SHOWN_THERMODYNAMICS_VERSION`, `PartitionThermodynamics` and the tests' switch
    `with_partition_thermodynamics`; `hadley_part_kg_m2_s` reads the air's bands. In `hydrology`,
    `ClimatePlaces`, `ShownAir`, `Means` and `ColumnAir`; `ColumnBudgets::of` takes
    `(grid, model, circulation)`, the zonal air and a `ColumnAir`. `climate` re-exports the hold and
    the switch to the tests.
  - _The figures behind it_ (research-r09-t13m-landing, not re-measured): at the reference locked
    Earth's substellar point the own record reads 343.0 K and the world shows 333.1 K (7–14 K over
    the day side); the partition's convergence summed to 1.48 times the evaporation (4.1 against
    2.78 mm/day, global means). Merlis and Schneider 2010 (arXiv:1001.5117, §3b, the 365-day case)
    is cited as that lane read it, not re-read here; its "≥ 40 mm/day" is a floor, and the
    substellar sea's 80 the plan's ceiling.
  - _The hold is not a `RecordRules` field_ (main asked for one, like R09.T7.g's): `RecordRules`
    carries what a system's records follow, and the coarse pass reads only its record
    (`CoarseInputs::for_body`), which holds nothing of the partition, so a field there would have no
    reader. The water cycle reads `PartitionThermodynamics::in_force` itself, as `ebm.rs` reads
    `GreenhouseConstants::in_force`. No `with_every_26_hold` yet: no test needs every 26 hold.
  - _The means_ are taken about each set's first value, so that cells at one temperature give it
    to the bit, as the brief's test asks. `ShownAir::of` asserts each cell's h and L q finite and
    one value a cell (the determinism review).
  - _Tests beyond the brief's_ (the reviews): `budget_in_air`'s split; the shown air at the
    columns' own temperatures giving their own budgets to the bit; under the switch, the level-5
    Earth's and the locked Earth's steps twice, interleaved (fast, 50 s), the small Earth's pass
    twice and in any order and not the held field (slow, about 1,000 s: a small-Earth pass takes
    114 s under either rule), and R09.T13.l's relief Earth (it passes).
  - _Below 26 every water cycle is R09.T13.m's to the bit_: the `earth_like` and
    `locked_earth_like` coarse goldens blessed on R09.T13.m's tree match.
  - _The rotating Earth's R09.T13.m tests_ run at the rule in force; with the hold lifted
    (a scratch tree) three tests fail (QUESTIONS to main, `findings.txt`): the flat continent's pin
    (25 arid against 12), the alike columns (the points' cell means' sampling noise: ψ 2.6%, E − P
    3.4 of the zonal field's largest) and T13.e's locked aquaplanet (its test world shows a flat
    281–297 K field, so the rain moves to 121° from the substellar point). A grid-native shown field,
    `Normalised::climate` plus the mean's constant, removes the noise (budgets within 7.2 × 10⁻¹²)
    and gives the locked Earth's figures within 0.4 mm/day, but not the continent's.
