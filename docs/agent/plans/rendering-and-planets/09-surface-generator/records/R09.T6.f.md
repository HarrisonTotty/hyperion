# R09.T6.f: records as built

The as-built records of R09.T6.f, moved from the Risks and open points of
[09-surface-generator.md](../../09-surface-generator.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **Deviations in R09.T6.f, as built** (2026-10-27; lane r09-channels, from `cdb906dc`;
  decision-r09-channel-term §4 and §8, with main's leans and rulings of 2026-10-27).
  - _Version._ The change is re-blessed at 23 with no bump, as a pre-24 golden change (main's
    ruling), and p14-wire-24's bless note lists it. At 23 the server serves no generated surface:
    `RecordInputs::inputs` answers `behind_the_gate` (not modelled) while `GENERATOR_VERSION` 23 is
    below `GENERATED_SURFACES_FROM` 24 (hyperion-server/src/surface/inputs.rs:83). Only the
    server's tests feed it reference worlds.
  - _(a) as built._ The fade belongs to the query point's coarse cell, not to the groove's. Each
    level's raw incision in a dry cell is multiplied by Π S(d ÷ w) over the edges and corners the
    cell shares with a wet cell:
    - d is the distance in the cell's face coordinates, w = ½ ÷ 2^(k − L) of its side (the level's
      wall width), and S the cubic smoothstep;
    - so every groove, whichever cell heads it, is zero on every shore, and a trunk into water
      shallows over the last half cell;
    - a wet cell's own trunk is dropped from the groove lists, so a lake's outflow cuts nothing in
      its dry receiver either (deferred, below);
    - every correction corner a wet cell shares is zero, and the dry cell's bubble carries the rest
      of its mean;
    - the crate-private `Shore`, `fade_width` and `smoothstep` are new, and `ChannelCache` holds
      each coarse cell's shore;
    - a dry cell with no wet edge or corner neighbour is not faded and keeps every corner. Its
      incision still moves where a wet cell's trunk within two king moves no longer reaches it,
      where a neighbour's faded mean enters a shared corner, and by (b). Unchanged to the bit are
      the worlds dry now and the airless ones (every cell carries an epoch code, or no runoff),
      and on a world wet now only ground with no wet cell within three king moves, no held trunk
      within two and no floor (below).
    - The fade is a device for continuity, not a law: a high coast's valley meets the water at
      the coast's height, which (b) limits and nothing zeroes.
  - _(b) as built._
    - `trunk_valley_m(cell, receiver, sea_level, body)` (crate-private) is `valley_depth` held to
      max(z − ℓ, 0), with ℓ from `base_level_m`.
    - `ChannelNetwork::trunk` stores it as the trunk's valley (its `below_top`), so the tributaries'
      inherited top follows it.
    - `trunk_depth_m` is the one min(max(valley, d_bf), G_max) that the groove, the closed form and
      `EpochValley` share.
    - The groove keeps its bankfull floor (G ≥ d_bf), as P2's second clause allows. On a dry cell,
      though, the floor (below) holds a river's last reach at the sea, so its bed meets the sea at
      sea level rather than about one flow depth below it as in a backwater reach (Lamb,
      Nittrouer, Mohrig and Shaw 2012, JGR Earth Surface 117, F01002; deferred).
    - The base level follows Powell 1875 (Exploration of the Colorado River of the West and its
      Tributaries, p. 203): the sea is "a grand base level, below which the dry lands cannot be
      eroded", and a lake a local one. Both citations were read by the science check.
    - Worlds dry now keep their epoch valleys. Worlds with no sea are not exempted, against
      §4(b)'s "untouched": the header carries no flag, and its sea level is the lowest cell's ground
      (Design note 7). So every trunk there is held to that ground and the floor binds there too,
      a weak but harmless bound. No test world has steady-state trunks without a sea
      (`mars_like_without_its_epoch` is a test helper). Lean, for main: keep it.
  - _The closed forms (main's ruling)._
    - The public `channel_variance(cell, body, finer_than)` is kept to the bit, so the coarse field
      does not move. It is the coarse pass's `σ_h` budget input (`relief/budget.rs`, `C_c`), T6.b's
      form from the cell alone.
    - The synthesis's own forms now read the receiver (`synthesised_terms`):
      `unresolved_channel_variance` (`omitted_variance`, `unresolved_variance`) and
      `channel_structure_function`. A wet cell has no terms, the trunk's valley is held, and the
      tributaries' β is less the hold.
    - They return `None` where the view does not hold the receiver of a cell with a steady-state
      trunk: one king move, inside the margin.
    - `level_terms_to` gains a `TrunkValley` argument.
    - The `height/closed_forms` golden did not move: the hold does not bind at its cells.
  - _The per-point floor (§4(b)'s fallback; main's acceptance of 2026-10-27)._
    - Why: the cap alone left 47 of the reference Earth's 3,200 random dry points (23 on the locked
      Earth) cut more than 10 m below the sea, deepest 819 m. Two causes: neighbours' trunk walls
      reaching into low coastal cells, and the mean correction's bubble beside deeply cut
      neighbours. Both happen where the base spline already dips towards the sea floor.
    - `Synthesiser::height_at` (`synth.rs`, outside the ruling's two files) takes the channels no
      lower than the sea on a dry cell not of a world dry now: h = max(h₀ + c, min(h₀, sea)). The
      height is flat at the sea where the floor binds, and h₀ where the ground lies below the sea
      without the channels.
    - It is continuous, and it moves no height except towards h₀, so the level bound holds and the
      floored height stays inside `contribution_range_m`. `levels/tests.rs` now checks the
      delivered heights.
    - It binds only where the sum lies below the sea and c < 0, so every other height is the sum
      to the bit. Its h₀ is the other contributions summed in their order, so a view whose flow is
      terminal everywhere gives the same h₀ to the bit (the sim test reads it so).
    - It does not overlap R10.T4.b's `synth.rs` edits, which landed before it.
    - _Against the brainstorm (for a ruling):_ the floor acts after each level's mean is removed and
      only raises the height. Where it binds, a cell's mean ground therefore stands above its
      coarse elevation, against "nothing may be added at a fine level that changes the mean at a
      coarse one". It is also a C⁰ clamp, flat at the sea, not band-limited. On the synthetic Earth
      it lifts 54 of 60 coastal cells' mean ground at the finest level, by 15.05 m on average and
      77.49 m at most (`measure_the_channels_by_water`). Main accepted the floor as the ruling's
      fallback; the brainstorm's rule is for the owner's ruling through main.

- **Deferred findings (2026-10-13 rule) (R09.T6.f)** (recorded, not ruled unless stated; each for
  "main"'s list).
  - _The coarse pass's channel budget overbooks._ This is main's ruling to defer; the owner is an R09
    follow-up that moves the coarse field at a later bump.
    - `channel_variance` gives a mean 55,814 m² on the reference Earth against the synthesis's
      15,569 m² (locked Earth 31,348 against 8,668), every level whole, over every sixteenth cell,
      unweighted.
    - Wet cells' bankfull channels give only 0.03 m² (0.11 m²) of it. The rest is (b)'s hold on
      inland valleys, which on an Earth stand a few hundred metres above the sea under 1.3 km
      valleys.
    - About 40,000 m² of `C_c` is booked and not drawn: 0.64% of `σ_h`², with `σ_h` 2,510 m. So
      the realised `σ_h` is about 0.3% under plan 14's.
  - _Lake mouths, a figure, not a blocker (main)._ 180 of the reference Earth's 2,804 dry axis
    points of trunks into water (locked Earth 80 of 2,949) lie on trunks into lakes above the sea.
    The channels cut them from at or above the lake to below its surface less bankfull, deepest
    455 m (306 m). The floor is the sea's: one that followed each lake's level would step at cell
    edges.
  - _The trunks' depth._ `valley_depth` has a median of 1.3 km on the reference Earth (p90 2.5 km,
    maximum 4.0 km; the ruling's probe D), for a science check at coarse `k_s`. The hold now
    bounds most inland valleys instead.
  - _The fade's share._ It removes 5.6% of the coastal cells' raw valley variance on the synthetic
    Earth (60 cells, `measure_the_channels_by_water`). The trunk level fades over half a cell
    towards every shore.
  - _ℓ is the sea level for a dry receiver._ A basin that drains through dry ground to a lake above
    the sea is held to the sea, and so is a trunk into a dry, terminal cell, a playa's pit, whose
    valley can be cut below its basin's floor by up to its whole `valley_depth`. Lean: ℓ is the
    receiver's ground where the receiver is dry and terminal (the coarse pass's base levels are the
    sea, terminal lakes and playas' pits, `erosion/branches.rs`).
  - _The floor's base level is the sea everywhere._ A dry depression below the sea keeps no channel
    cut where its ground lies below the sea: the hold leaves only the bankfull channel, and the
    floor takes that too, leaving only the correction's lift. A trunk into a lake below the sea is
    held to that lake, but its valley is flattened at the sea. A floor at each closed basin's own
    base level would step at cell edges, as for lakes (above).
  - _Dry ground's variance of z_ at band levels 3 and 4 stays 8.42 and 2.56 on the reference Earth
    after the fix (locked Earth 1.69 and 1.58), against about 1 in wet cells. For R10.T15.b.
  - _A river's last reach_ meets the sea at sea level on the floor, not one flow depth below it as
    in a backwater reach (Lamb et al. 2012; above).
  - _The closed forms omit_ the neighbours' trunks reaching into a cell (dry cells, ×2), the fade
    and the floor. With the hold, this now shows in R10.T5's readings. A coastal cell's own trunk
    term shrinks, while its neighbours' reaching trunks and the floor's flats stay realised. So
    the reference Earth's var z at its 1000 m survey rose from 1.1584 to 1.3568, with RMS ÷ σ
    1.0072, and `STYLE_GAP_Z` is re-pinned to 1.36 (main's ruling). The owner is a later R09
    follow-up, in which the closed forms count the neighbours' trunks that reach in. R10.T15.b
    re-measures at 24.
  - _A lake's outflow_ has no groove from its shore to its receiver's key point: a wet cell's own
    trunk cuts nothing.
  - _The floor flattens_ dry ground at the sea level where the channels would cut through it, a
    flat and not a drowned valley.
