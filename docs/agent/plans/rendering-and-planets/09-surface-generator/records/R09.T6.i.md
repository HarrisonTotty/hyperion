# R09.T6.i: records as built

The as-built records of R09.T6.i, the wet-now tributaries' valleys, of
[09-surface-generator.md](../../09-surface-generator.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same
form.

- **Deviations in R09.T6.i, as built** (2026-10-31; lane r09-t6i, on R09.T6.h at
  `GENERATOR_VERSION` 24). It rides 26 behind a hold (main's rule), not the draft's plain bump:
  nothing moves at 24 or 25. Parts (a)–(e) are committed separately, in the order (a), (c), (d),
  (b), (e), then the closed forms.
  - _The hold, for 26's bump to delete:_ `TRIBUTARIES_VERSION` (26), `ChannelRules` and its `Held`
    arm, the doors under `testing` (`FieldHeader::with_channel_rules`,
    `CoarseField::with_channel_rules`, the sim's `coarse_pass_under`, the crate-private
    `Pass::with_channel_rules`) and the tripwire `the_tributaries_wait_for_their_version`. The rules
    ride the field's header by value, off the wire, set from `GENERATOR_VERSION` when it is built or
    decoded; the coarse pass writes the rules it booked `C_c` under into its field's header, and
    outside `testing` checks them against the version's by a debug assertion only.
  - _26's movers_ (measured with the rules in force at 24): the wet worlds' surface goldens (the
    height, parts, closed-form, baked-patch and playa goldens of `synth::height_golden`, `bake::`,
    `ground::`, `material::`, `material::weights::`, the albedo worlds', `worker::`), the sim's
    Earth and locked Earth coarse goldens (both tests of each) and `level-bounds.json`; and 11 tests
    that pin R09.T6.h's wet network, to restate. No Mars-like, Moon, Ceres or Europa golden moves.
  - _(a)_ φ is computed once (`Segment::accord`) and carried in `Kind::Tributary`; R_head is
    rise(L_heads, L_k) where L_k exceeds the heads' main stream, else 0.
  - _(b)_ `WET_WALL_PER_DEPTH` = `STEEPEST_WALL` ÷ `VALLEY_WALL_GRADIENT` (2.5), no new figure.
    Every wet groove is bent, so T6.h's `Wall::Fixed` (a wet trunk's half-cell wall) goes.
  - _(c)_ The join's shift runs along the parent's whole course, held to its ends, not to the
    chosen sub-chord (held to a sixteenth of a chord, most joins clamped). `Nearest::beats` breaks
    a tie at a shared vertex by the chord's span.
  - _(d)_ A wet trunk grades to its receiver's valley (`onward_valley_m`), ungraded into water. A
    tributary is bent as one half (`Bend::tributary`), keyed by `surface_cell` of its own cell,
    words k − 1, and held to no cell (`Held::NONE`): its course ends on its parent's, which may lie
    in another cell; its reads stay inside the margin. _The look's choice:_ `BEND_FACTOR` of its own
    chord at every level, not quartered per level (quartered drew straight thorns at 150 m, the
    angles' IQR 4°). _Further:_ each wet sub-chord reads its depth at the point's projection on its
    own line (`Ends::OnLine`), which removes the scallops (up to 19.6 m) where the depth falls fast;
    a world dry now keeps T6.h's nearest point.
  - _(e)_ `Continuity`, memoised per coarse cell in `NetworkMemo`; a corner cell with no
    tributaries (one under water among them) counts steepness 0 and its cut at L, so a tributary
    fades towards the coast within a cell. `cut_area_m2` inverts the width and discharge in closed
    form, its ratio held at its fitted ends. _Reads:_ the segments below a coarse cell read four
    king moves (a wet trunk's grade reads its receiver's receiver), the height function five: the
    margin itself, with no slack.
  - _The closed forms_ (`bent_level_terms_to`, `wet_trunk_term`, `Moments::linear`). A tributary
    level's G is linear from its join's (the parent level's mean) to its head's, min(R_head,
    G_join − its own rise), held to the channel's depth and the level's cap, weighted by the cell's
    continuous cut. The linear profile stands in for the concave one (⟨G⟩ low by 2–6% of the
    rise), which the variance share absorbs.
  - _The constants_ (`measure_the_bent_network_s_constants`, by hand): `MEAN_BENT_TRIBUTARY_LENGTH`
