# R09.T6.h: records as built

The as-built records of R09.T6.h, the dry-now trunks bent and graded, of
[09-surface-generator.md](../../09-surface-generator.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same
form.

- **Deviations in R09.T6.h, as built** (2026-10-30; lane r09-t6h-bend, on R09.T6.g re-cut at
  `GENERATOR_VERSION` 24; main's rulings of 2026-10-30). Re-blessed on the lane at 24; main
  re-blesses at 25's bump commit.
  - _Ruling 1 (decision-r09-t14-mars-ceiling §5 amended)._ "Along a trunk's second chord, G grades
    by a smoothstep from its own cell's to its receiver's, never outside the two; each wall 5 G at
    every point." Recorded in T6.c's entry too.
  - _New public API_ (`synth/channels/bend.rs`, `incision.rs`): `BEND_PARTS` (16), `BEND_FACTOR`
    (0.1), `EPOCH_TRUNK_SINUOSITY` (1.05), `Segment::bend_m`, `EpochValley::graded_void_m2` and
    `trunk_void_m2`, `GRADED_SAMPLES`; the sim's `dry_now_lowering_m(valley, receiver_m, d, w)`.
    Tag `surface.channel.bend`.
  - _D = 16 as two Dendry passes, 4 then 4_ (the look's choice, against the lean D = 8): one pass of
    8 left a sawtooth of about 30° kinks every 2.5 km at 150 m; the second pass's spline runs
    through the first's points, its factor quartered. It costs about a quarter more in the groove.
  - _The spline_ is Catmull–Rom with its tangents scaled to the chord (the uniform form where the
    chords are equal), so that a short chord between long ones does not overshoot.
  - _The confluence rule_ (a deviation from Dendry): the course's own inflow is the largest, by
    drainage and then by the higher packed key, among the edge neighbours that drain into the cell,
    shed runoff and lie out of the water; a wet donor, like a wet receiver, gives Dendry's
    reflected point.
  - _Grading (lean, 2026-10-30)._ A receiver with no epoch valley (`UNCUT`, terminal, under water)
    has G_j = 0, so the valley tapers to nothing at its key point. A trunk whose own G is 0 draws
    nothing, as before, so the closure's d_i cap holds.
  - _The groove_ is the maximum over its sub-chords' grooves, each at its own nearest point's G and
    W (research-r09-earth-look's finding): no step crosses a bend (`no_step_crosses_a_bend`).
  - _Reads._ A dry-now trunk reads its cell, its receiver and its four edge neighbours: one king
    move, not the brief's four (the receiver's crossing needs only its record and key points). The
    incision's reach stays four, inside the margin's five.
  - _Closed forms._ `drawn_channel_variance` and the structure function take a dry-now trunk's
    graded second half (reading its receiver), `channel_variance` its own G (cell alone); both
    times `EPOCH_TRUNK_SINUOSITY`. The closure reads the receiver on the solve's last routing, the
    synthesis the final routing's, so they differ per cell (p10 −36%) where the final routing
    turns a trunk into a late crater; (A2) agrees to 0.3%.
  - _For R09.T6.i._ `Bend::new`, `Groove::bent`, `Wall` and `Depth::Graded` take any trunk; the
    world test (`is_epoch`) is only in `ChannelNetwork::trunk`. The tributary search offers a bent
    trunk's 2D sub-chords in course order (`Segment::chord`), reached by no world yet: pin a
    tributary joining a bent trunk in a golden when wet trunks bend. `height/graded` pins the
    capped-wall forms no golden world reaches.
  - _Goldens moved_ (x86_64): `height/{mars_like_points, mars_like_parts, bakes, closed_forms}`'s
    Mars-like entries (`closed_forms` gains a Mars-like "held" cell, the graded drawn form now
    under the cell-alone one), `ground/mars_like`, `material/mars_like`, `weights/mars_like`, new
    `height/bends`; the sim's `planetary/surface/mars_like` and `rng/tags` (the tag's line); the
    client's `level-bounds.json` (its `mars_like` entry). No Earth, locked Earth, Moon, Ceres or
    Europa golden moved.
  - _Som et al.'s spans._ Their 160–885 km are valley lengths, so the whole-course check takes the
    courses 160–885 km long (median 1.427; 1.351 unbent); the research's 1.263 took the spans.
  - _Benches._ The Mars-like world joins `surface/point` and `surface/patch` at 11 and 17 on a
    trunk's key point, and native `surface/channels` (`mars/through-7`); WebAssembly runs
    `surface/point` alone.

--features testing --test trunk_bend_worlds --cargo-profile slow-test --profile slow
