# R09.T6.g: records as built

The as-built records of R09.T6.g, moved from the Risks and open points of
[09-surface-generator.md](../../09-surface-generator.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **The entry and its route** (main's ruling, 2026-10-29, on the RM5 close-out's third question;
  not yet built). decision-r09-channel-term §9's follow-up gets this entry, aimed at 25's train. It
  rides a plain bump: built on its lane branch, picked just before the bump commit and re-blessed
  in it (decision-bump-plan-after-22 §1.4). No surface-crate hold is built now.
  - _Why no hold._ `valley_depth` (`synth/channels/incision.rs`) runs in both halves: in the sim's
    coarse pass, through `drawn_valley` (a world dry now's epoch valleys) and through
    `channel_variance` into σ_h's budget (`C_c`); and in the synthesis, on the server and in the
    client's module, through `trunk_valley_m`, the groove and the closed forms. A hold must switch
    both halves together, or a field whose σ_h was budgeted under one law is drawn under the other.
    - A thread-local switch fails determinism in the tests. Whole coarse passes are shared
      `OnceLock` fixtures in both crates (`relief/tests/sigma_h.rs`, `synth/test_support.rs` among
      them), and a fixture first built inside the switch is built under it, whichever test reaches
      it first.
    - A hold carried by value costs more than it saves here: a new in-memory member of
      `FieldHeader`, a type the gate freezes; the rules in `ChannelBody`; a fork at more than
      decision-galaxy-25-train E4's three call sites (`valley_depth`, `drawn_valley`,
      `channel_variance`, the closed forms and `base_level_m`); and both arms in the client's
      module until the bump.
    - Nothing waits on it. No later plan names `incision.rs`, `channels.rs`, `valley_depth` or
      `channel_variance`; R10.T15.b re-measures after it only if it has landed; and at 25 the only
      other mover of its goldens is R09.T7.g's swap, which moves them only in the bump commit.
  - _When to revisit:_ if 25's bump slips past T6.g's landing by weeks and the branch needs a
    re-port, if a second synthesis rider appears for the same bump, or if a task needs the new
    depth on integration.
  - _The hold, if one is then chosen._ Carried by the field's header and keyed on the generator
    version, never thread-local:
    - a `SurfaceRules { channels: ChannelRules }` value (`Held | Trunks`), with `TRUNKS_VERSION`
      25 beside the code it holds and `of_version(v)` making one comparison a field;
    - `FieldHeader` holding it privately, off the wire, set from `of_version(generator_version())`
      by `FieldHeader::new` and by the wire's `read_header`, with a door under the `testing`
      feature for the tests; `ChannelBody::of(header)` copying it, and the sim's `Pass` carrying
      the same value from `of_version(GENERATOR_VERSION)`;
    - the held arm verbatim, one `match` at each of at most three entries, no fitted table
      restamped, no payload layout or protocol move, and a tripwire on `TRUNKS_VERSION`;
    - its determinism tests: an extension golden over trunk, coastal, lake and playa cells at band
      levels 5, 7 and 19 on both Earths and the Mars-like world, blessed at 23 before the hold and
      unchanged by it; movers under the 25 rules, with §9.2's targets holding; the rules agreeing
      across `FieldHeader::new`, a decoded header and the pass; the same seed twice to the bit;
      and the new arm with explicit rules in `just test-wasm-fast` and `just test-wasm-slow`.

- **Deviations in R09.T6.g, as built** (2026-10-30; lane r09-t6g-depth; decision-r09-channel-term
  §9). Re-blessed on the lane at 23, no bump and no hold; main re-blesses at the bump commit.
  - _(a)._ `valley_depth` is unchanged: the coarse `k_s` is about 70 m^0.9 at every drainage from
    10³ to 10⁶ km², so the index, not a trunk's drainage, is the cause. New public
    `VALLEY_RELIEF_SHARE` (0.5): `trunk_valley_m` = min(`valley_depth`, 0.5 max(z − ℓ, 0)). Worlds
    dry now are unchanged to the bit (`EpochValley`'s bound reads `valley_depth`), so T6.h measures
    on the old steady-state bound.
  - _(b)._ New public `synth::channels::drawn_channel_variance`, of a closure over the coarse
    records, the cell, the sea level, the body and the wavelength. `budget::book` books it;
    `channel_variance` is kept, the cell-alone form, booked by nothing.
  - _(c)._ The synthesis's trunk level counts halves of `MEAN_TRUNK_LENGTH` ÷ 2: the cell's own,
    faded (⟨S⟩ ½, ⟨S²⟩ 13 ÷ 35) into a wet receiver, and each dry donor's, unfaded. A dry terminal
    cell carries its donors' halves. Not modelled: the donors' fade towards other wet edges, and the
    floor.
  - _(d)._ `base_level_m`: a dry, terminal receiver's ground, or the sea where that is higher. The
    floor (crate-private `Channels::floor_at`) is bilinear between corners, each the highest pit
    level of the cells sharing it, never below the sea; it lifts some points of the basins'
    neighbours. Dry basins below the sea keep T6.f's sea floor (P2).
  - _Contract (R10)._ The closed forms also answer `None` where a cell that water drains through
    lacks an edge neighbour. The worker's `LevelSigma` waits for such a cell's margin (`pending`).
  - _Fixture._ The worker's Earth-like patch moved to (896, 675) by its recorded search.
