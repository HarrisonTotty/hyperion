- **Deviations in T16, as built** (2026-10-23; lane r09-t16, on `898a3c94`; `GENERATOR_VERSION`
  stays 23 and `SURFACE_PAYLOAD_FORMAT` 1, main's ruling: the serve gate is shut, so the goldens

  - _Where the goldens are written._ In the library, `src/planetary/surface/coarse_golden.rs`
    (`cfg(test)`), the files `tests/golden/planetary/surface/<world>.golden`, after T9's precedent
    (_Where the goldens are written_ in "Deviations in T9, as built"): R04's wasip1 runs build
    `-p hyperion-sim` without its `testing` feature, so an integration test behind it, as
    `tests/surface_coarse_golden.rs` is, compiles to nothing there. That file instead checks that
    each world of `sample_worlds()` gives its golden's payload digest (the fast worlds in one test,
    the two slow ones a test each), which ties R10's worlds to the pinned fields.
  - _What a golden pins_ (4.4–6.9 kB each). The world (level and counts); every member of the
    header's parts and of its crater contract and spectrum; every palette entry's every member (a
    liquid's band arrays by digest); one digest per `SynthesisCell` member, with summaries (the
    elevation's range, the cells by crust, boundary kind and class, under water and with ice, the
    crater state's range and median, and the epoch incision's `NONE`, `UNCUT` and cut counts with
    the cut codes' p10, p50, p90 and maximum); one digest per climate member; the coarse craters'
    count and one digest per member, the degradation bytes' with their range (R09.T7.j's
    determinism ask); the whole-field payload's blocks, bytes and digest, each block's version
    bytes zeroed as `wire/payloads` does; and on the two worlds wet now T12.a's three bands of the
    final land hypsometry. A pattern with no `..` lists each record's members, so that a new member
    fails to compile until it is pinned.
  - _Mars is fast_ (7.1 s a pass in the test profile), where the entry has it slow, so that R09.T6.c's
    outputs are pinned in every `ci` on both targets: its codes (`NONE` 0, `UNCUT` 70,414, cut
    27,890; cut codes p10 / p50 / p90 / max 96 / 130 / 152 / 202) and, through the elevation and
    payload digests, the closure. Ceres, the Moon and the Europa are fast; the Earth (about 100 s)
    and the locked Earth (about 22 s) slow.
  - _The two sample worlds are in `reference`_, not built in `tests/common/surface_worlds.rs` as
    plan 10 has them (its Files, about line 430), so that the library's goldens and R10's tests
    share one definition. `europa_like` (level 6, 0.55 s a pass): plan 14's rules for the measured
    Europa wherever one is built (its relief, airless ice temperature at Bond 0.35, airless
    equator–pole profile, heat flow, resurfacing age of 60 Myr, impactors with `R_b` 5.73 at 24.2
    km/s), an ice shell whose water-ice crust is its lid, no ice cover listed. `locked_earth_like`
    (level 8, 22.4 s): the reference Earth locked 1:1 to an M3.5V dwarf (0.27 M☉, 10^−2.03 L☉,
    Mamajek's table) at the Earth's mean flux, 0.0966 au, a year of 21.10 d (the climate tests'
    `locked_earth` takes 24 d from memory), one month, plan 14's contrasts for its Earth under the
    lock (32.9 K equator–pole, 59.8 K day–night). The science check (2026-10-23) found no blocker
    and corrected three citations. So plan 10's tiers differ: Mars is fast and the locked world
    slow, where plan 10 has Mars slow and both built worlds fast, so R10.T1.b's locked-world ice
    case runs in the slow tier, and each R10 binary that runs the fast worlds pays about 7 s for

- **Main's rulings on R09.T16's cost findings** (decision-r09-pass-cost, 2026-10-23). None is a
  blocker under the owner's rule of 2026-10-20, and T16 lands as built.
  - _The 94.9 s pass breaks no contract._ It runs as one bulk job on the pool, off the runtime,
    shared by one flight and kept in the field cache. `surface_field` waits for it, but the
    terrain's store asks through the link's own request client, which has no timer. The client's
    `REQUEST_TIMEOUT_MS` of 30 s belongs to `useServerRequest`, for the displays' requests, and
    the server sets no deadline. Meanwhile the body has no coverage, so it stays on R07's disc
    (R10.T10.e) with `SURVEY COVERAGE: PENDING` (R10.T12): a stated wait, not a wrong picture.
    At version 23 no generated body runs a pass (the serve gate is shut until 24). The
    brainstorm's "tens of seconds" and decision-r09-t14-choices C's 60 s are recorded references,
    both missed; C's 60 s guarded the σ_h iteration, whose fallback T14.d took.
  - _The climate step's 68.9 s is not R09.T13.i's G runs._ Those are inside the 31.8 s quiet at
    which T13.i landed, and seed 3's 64–73 s is another world's, measured before the change below.
    Both steps rose between `01674b8c` (T14.d) and `898a3c94` (this task's base): the climate
    from 31.8 to 68.9 s, and the erosion from 10.50 to 17.88 s, though it runs no G run. In that
    range only P14.T54.a's 22 → 23 batch touches the climate's code or the reference worlds'
    inputs (R09.T6.c's change to the erosion is its dry-now branch, and other plan 14 commits
    change only the registry that the climate reads). The batch's phase-data switch (`032a8c5f`)
    makes every saturation pressure T49.b's curve: water's six `libm::pow`, an `ln` and an `exp`
    a call, where the held curve took one `exp`. The erosion's two water balances make up to
    some 28 million such calls a pass, which fits its rise. The energy balance reads its water
    terms from tables, so the climate's rise is not attributed. It is a cost of ruled physics,
    measured late: not a bug and not a ruled cost. R09.T16.a attributes it. P14.T53.a is not part
    of it: it is not in this task's tree, and at version 23 `GreenhouseConstants::in_force` gives
    the held constants, once a body.
  - _A pass's 408 MB_ (the process's peak, its 22 MB floor included) is no crash risk at the
    passes the client asks for, the bodies near a view's camera (one or two). Fifteen at once is
    not reachable today. R09.T17.a bounds the passes in flight, which also keeps a pool worker
    free for the displays' requests.
  - _The fast order test's 909.5 s and the bytes test's 248.7 s_ stay in the fast tier as built,
