# R09.T7.g: records as built

The as-built records of R09.T7.g, moved from the Risks and open points of
[09-surface-generator.md](../../09-surface-generator.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **Deviations in R09.T7.g, as built** (2026-10-28; lane `r09-t7g`, the pure half: the task, its
  table and its reader; science-r09-drainage.md §8.13, `signoff-12.md` item 13).
  - _Two halves._ The task `rough_plains_cold_traps`, its table `tables::rough_plains_cold_traps`
    (since 23) and the reader `cold_trap::rough_plains` landed at 23 with no bump, read by nothing
    generated. `cold_trap`'s `fraction`, `fractions` and `permanent_shadow` still take the plains
    as the labelled half-normal bowls with `PLAINS_DEPTH_SCALE` and `PLAINS_SURROUNDINGS`. The swap
    is still to build (_For the swap_, below).
  - _The surfaces._ Eight periodic 512² surfaces, modes from 2 to 32 cycles across (16 pixels to
    the shortest wave) of amplitude |k|^−1.9 and random phase, summed by the task's own radix-2 FFT
    in a fixed order. Heights are scaled to a directional RMS slope of 1 over the facets' forward
    differences, the slopes the bilinear rays meet, and a plain of slope w is that surface times w:
    horizon tangents scale exactly with w, so one trace serves every slope. Each mode's phase is
    one draw of the fit crate's counter-based `Draws` (the displaced forms' and the atmosphere
    tracer's), keyed by the manifest's seed salted with `ROUGH_PLAINS_STREAM` (`roughpln`), the
    surface's number and the mode's signed wavenumbers, so a surface is the same on every grid that
    holds its modes (the determinism audit's catch: a key of kx mod N made grids incomparable). No
    domain tag: the fit is not generated output.
  - _The shadow._ Horizons at 180 azimuths (2°) by rays of 1-pixel steps to 256 pixels. A pixel is
    in permanent shadow exactly when every azimuth's horizon has a declination above D = δ + α. Up
    each vertical the declination is a sinusoid in the elevation, so where e₀ + D < 90° the least
    over the visible sky is the horizon's or below −D. Each pixel's least horizon declination
    serves every D, and a test holds it to the star's path at D at every azimuth.
  - _The irradiance._ It is first order. Each sunlit facet is a Lambertian source of its direct
    flux S μ, seen through the shadowed pixel's view factors, (Δφ ÷ π) ∫ max(0, n · d) cos a da
    over each visible piece of the profile along every other azimuth (4°), with the pixel's own
    tilted normal n. A piece is its nearest pixel's facet, lit when the star is above that pixel's
    own horizon. The peak is over the star's path at D, sampled at every other azimuth (4°). At most
    4,096 shadowed pixels a surface and node are traced, every k-th in raster order.
    - On spherical bowls at the pole it gives Hayne et al.'s eq. 6 (0.7566 against 0.7562 at d/D
      0.1, 0.8915 against 0.8876 at 0.2) and f (1 − f) S sin e, the first order of Ingersoll et
      al.'s cavity balance, in the mean 2.5% and 3.4% low.
    - A horizontal receiver instead of the pixel's own normal (a diagnostic on a 128² pilot) raised
      water 1.0–2.6 times at w 0.1–0.5 and matched neither §8.13's water nor its CO₂.
  - _The table's layout_ (item 13 fixed its quantity, the geometric irradiance with no albedo or
    emissivity, but not its grid):
    - seven slopes, w 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.5, 0.7 and 1;
    - `SHADOW` on a fine grid: e₀ ÷ w (radians) at 33 nodes on [0, 4] and D ÷ w at 33 on [0, 1.6];
    - `COLD` on every second colatitude and every fourth limb node (17 × 9): the shares of each
      node's shadow whose peak irradiance is at most 0 and 16 fractions of S sin e, 10⁻⁴ to 10,
      even in their logarithm;
    - `INSTANTANEOUS` too, 41 nodes of tan e ÷ w on [0, 2], so that Smith's test runs against the
      reader;
    - shares rounded to four significant figures; the file is 170 kB.

    The shadow falls about three times every 0.2 of D ÷ w near the pole, so a linear read at a
    pilot's 0.2 spacing overstated it by 15–25% inside a cell. The cold shares change slowly and
    are the run's cost. A point's cold share is its trilinear shadow times the shadow-weighted mean
    of the eight cold nodes' shares about it, so it never exceeds the shadow.

  - _The read, beyond the text._ Each slope node `w_n` reads its cold shares at the fraction times
    (`w_n` ÷ w)²: at fixed ratios the irradiance over S sin e scales as w² (a spherical bowl's
    f (1 − f) ≈ 4γ²), so a slope below 0.05 reads the 0.05 slice at its own ratios and at its own
    scaled fraction (the science check's finding; the plan's "reads the 0.05 slice" said nothing
    of the fraction). Between irradiance nodes a share is read linear in the logarithms of the
    fraction and the share where both are positive, so a tail ∝ x^p is read exactly; linear in
    ln x overstated a p = 2 tail by up to 1.33 times at these nodes.
  - _S sin e_ takes e = min(90°, e₀ + D): the star is a point at its upper limb for the shadow and
    for the lit facets alike, as traced (`irradiance_limit`). T7.d's bowls take the disk's
    flux-weighted elevation at e₀ + δ instead. At the Moon's pole under the real Sun the normaliser
    is sin 1.81° against sin 1.54°, 1.18 times. (lean, 2026-10-28)
  - _Public items beyond the text._
    - The reader: `permanent_shadow`, `cold_share`, `instantaneous_shadow`, `irradiance_limit`,
      `shadow_at` and `fraction` in `cold_trap::rough_plains`. `shadow_at` and `fraction` take a
      slope, not a `Terrain`, and `fraction` no conduction share. They refuse a slope outside
      [0, π/4], a colatitude outside [0, π/2], D outside [0, π), a NaN fraction, an elevation
      outside [0, π/2), a threshold that is not finite and positive and a latitude past a pole
      (`tests/panics.rs`, inside its `cold_trap` module).
    - The fitting module: `Params`, `Surface`, `Horizons`, `Axes`, `RoughPlainsFit` (whose read is
      the reader's, the same arithmetic in the same order: a fitting-crate test holds the two to
      the bit over 11 slopes, 10 colatitudes, 8 limbs and 10 fractions), `fit`, `RunCounts`,
      `figures`, `Figures`, `BandFigures`, `smith_points`, `smith_shadow`, `render`,
      `CHECK_SLOPES`, `SLOPE_MIN`, `SLOPE_MAX`, `ROUGH_PLAINS_STREAM` and `VERSION`.
    - The task is slow: `check` holds it to its inputs and body hashes, and the integration test
      re-renders the committed values.
    - The golden `cold_trap/rough_plains` pins the reader's bits on all three targets (an
      extension at 23).
  - _The run_ (pod, 4 vCPU, not the target's): 49.4 min wall, 177.8 CPU-min, 1.23 GB peak resident
    set. The trace keeps only the horizon tangents (377 MB a 512² surface) and re-marches each
    sampled pixel's views; a first build that stored every pixel's pieces peaked at 12.6 GB. One
    and four threads, and chunks of 1, 3 and 4, give the same table (the unit and integration
    tests).
  - _Convergence._
    - The same eight surfaces at 256² (8 pixels to the shortest wave; pod, 4 vCPU, 12.9 min wall,
      44.2 CPU-min) keep the shadow within 2% of 512²'s (2.64%, 12.84%, 21.93% and 33.51% over
      80–90° at w 0.1, 0.2, 0.3 and 0.5). Water is 0.5–8% lower (2.00%, 5.21%, 3.82% and 1.92%), CO₂
      8–19% lower (0.089%, 0.045%, 0.033% and 0.027%), and Smith's fit within 10.7%. The shadow is
      converged in the grid; the cold traps rise with it, by under a tenth a doubling for water and
      a fifth for CO₂, toward §8.13's water and away from its CO₂.
    - Before the signed key (pilots of 2 surfaces whose grids differed in their kx < 0 modes),
      widening the spectrum to 2–64 cycles at 256² raised water 0–22% and CO₂ 1.3–3 times: the cold
      tail depends on the spectrum's range, a modelling choice.
    - Halving the view and path steps to 2° moved CO₂ 2–10% and water 1–3%.
