# R09.T16.a: records as built

The as-built records of R09.T16.a, moved from the Risks and open points of
[09-surface-generator.md](../../09-surface-generator.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **Main's ruling on R09.T16.a's bit-moving proposals** (decision-r09-climate-levers, 2026-10-23;
  one ruling for the figure, its outcomes ruled in advance). The climate's rise is τ's fit:
  `840e319a`'s partition greenhouse under P14.T57.a's constants slows its relaxation from 19,910
  to 69,128 sweeps over the climate's 53 steady states (the fit's 51 probes and the zonal
  search's two slope probes), about 375 to 1,300 each against the cap of 2,000. At the head, 35 s
  of the fit's 37.5 s are calls of the saturation curve, which its probes evaluate directly. The
  zonal search's runs and G1 take a few more orbits, I13's as before. After R09.T16.a's levers,
  bit for bit (B4, the shared-log power, and B3 with B9), the fit should take about 13–14 s, the
  climate step about 40–45 s and the pass about 60–65 s (the ruling's estimates from the lane's
  figures, not timed).
  - _Taken: τ's fit on a table_ (R09.T16.b; a rider of version 24 if its commit lands before
    P14.T54.b.i's, else of 25). It removes the fit's remaining curve calls and keeps its start,
    stop, cap, bisection and fold guard. Estimated: the fit about 3–4 s, the climate step
    about 30–35 s (back in R09.T13.i's landed band), and the pass about 50–55 s.
  - _Refused: a warm start._ The fit solves annual-mean steady states on the whole zonal grid, so
    the only warm start is the previous probe. Continuing from it follows a branch past its fold,
    where the isothermal start at T\* falls into the other basin. That changes the fold guard's
    candidates and the branch kept (R09.T13.i's relief and sea-level Earths are `Folded`, a
    snowball or capped state against a warm one).
  - _Refused: loosening the 10⁻⁹ K stop._ At a ratio of about 0.985 a sweep, a state lies within
    about 70 times its last change of its fixed point: about 7 × 10⁻⁸ K at 10⁻⁹ K and 7 × 10⁻⁵ K
    at 10⁻⁶ K. Through the warm branch's slope (about 200 K per unit of u), either moves u₀ by
    under 10⁻⁶. That is far inside the search's 0.01 K stop, the climate's 0.1 K and the
    field's step of 0.01 K, and R09.T13.d imposes the mean exactly, so the published fields
    cannot tell the two apart. The stop's work is the fold guard's. A transient lingering in a
    fold's bottleneck meets the stop over a band of u in proportion to the tolerance, so 10⁻⁶ K
    would widen that band about a thousandfold (decision-r09-t13i's prototype fitted 288.000 K
    from such a transient). The saving is only logarithmic, about 470 of 1,300 sweeps
    (ln 1,000 ÷ 0.0146), and about 1 s on the table. The stop and the cap stay.
  - _Deferred: an accelerated relax_ (Aitken or Anderson on the relaxation, or a smaller
    `RELAXATION`). On the table the sweeps cost about 3 s, and an extrapolated or mixed step can
    cross the separatrix between branches, as the seasonal runs' jumps needed a reject rule to
    avoid. It joins I13's candidates after 24, only where a measured world's fit dominates its
    climate step.
  - _Refused: a shared saturation table, or τ's fit tabulated._ A table held across calls is a
    cache. It would move every caller of the registry's curve beyond R09's fit (the erosion's
    balances, the water cycle and plan 14's partition), and a tabulated fit is a surrogate model.
- **Deviations in T16.a, as built** (2026-10-23; lane r09-t17a, built on `71532a17` and gated
  merged with `762073e7`, one batch and one gate with R09.T17.a; decision-r09-climate-levers §7).
  No contract, golden or version moves: every lever is bit for bit, each held to the path it
  replaces. The levers' figures were taken on `71532a17`.
  - _The attribution's runs._ Step 1 (`coarse/erosion_l8` and a probe at `032a8c5f` and at its
    parent `843b82af`, two rounds, the second reversed) ran under the heavy lock, one thread a
    pass, `CPUQuota` 1200%, at load 7.0–22.9 and I/O full avg10 at most 6, by main's leave
    without a quiet window, since it compares two commits run side by side. Step 2 (the probe at
    `898a3c94` and `71532a17` in two rounds, and the bench by step at `71532a17`) ran at load
    1.0–5.1. The governor was schedutil. The old commits were built and run in a detached timing
    worktree, seeded from the seed source and removed after.
  - _A probe beside the bench._ The probe (R09.T13.i's pattern: inserted, built and deleted) ran
    in the test profile. It timed the climate's model (τ's fit, the zonal search, G1, G2, the
    runs' tables), its water cycle (the sea's rates) and the rest, and the erosion's two water
    balances, and counted the curve's calls and relax's sweeps. At `71532a17` the profiles agree
    on the climate step (68.7 s in the test profile, 69.3–69.8 s in release, 67.1 s in the bench's
    by-step run), so the climate's 31.8 → 68.9 s compares like with like. The erosion is set
    against T14.d's and T16's in the bench profile only (the test profile's 20.0 s against
    release's 17.8 s).
  - _No halving of the range._ The switch accounts for the erosion's rise: `coarse/erosion_l8`
    takes 11.038 s at `843b82af` (10.891–11.218) and 19.070 s at `032a8c5f` (18.453–19.810), +8.0 s
    against the +7.4 s of 10.504 → 17.881 s. So E2's halving of `01674b8c..898a3c94` was not run.
  - _The climate's commit by counts._ Step 2 put the climate's rise in τ's fit, whose sweeps are a
    deterministic count: 19,041 at `032a8c5f`, 69,571 at `898a3c94`. So its commit was found
    untimed: the probe at E2's first-listed `840e319a` and at its parent `bdd1f73f` (19,910 →
    69,128 sweeps; the fit 7.42 → 36.40 s), through `build-slot` at `CPUQuota` 400% and load about
    2, not under the heavy lock.
  - _`PowBase`, a port of `libm`'s `pow`._ `crates/hyperion-base/src/math/pow_base.rs`, a module
    of its own under `math`, public as `hyperion_base::math::PowBase`, outside this plan's crates:
    `libm` 0.2.16's `pow` transcribed line for line and split where its logarithm stage ends,
    which reads x alone; every input outside that stage's domain is `libm::pow` itself. `NOTICE`
    gains `libm`'s MIT licence and the fdlibm notice the file keeps (main's approval,
    2026-10-23). Its tests hold it to `libm::pow` to the bit natively, on wasip1 and in the
    browser's V8.
  - _Where the levers sit._ B4: `evaporation::{month_terms, open_months, ClimateCellTerms}`, for
    `hydrology::Evaporation::each_open_cell` and `after_erosion::NetLoss::at_water_surfaces`, each
    making its own memo. The shared logarithm: every phase-diagram equation's sum in
    `substance/diagram.rs`. B3 and B9: `ebm.rs`'s `Saturations`, `direct_reads`, `column_reads`
    and `DirectWets`, and `Tabulated`'s nodes. Each replaced path stays as the tests' oracle,
    under `cfg(test)` or copied into a test.
  - _What they share._ The small Earth's τ fit makes 12,019,203 curve calls against 31,918,131; a
    run's table half its calls (214,218 → 107,109); B4's memo answers 3,150 of the lakes' net
    loss's 6,144 cells after the climate (957 of 1,536 climate cells at one water surface) and
    3,150 of the sea rates' 4,362.
  - _Not built: the curve's per-call constants hoisted out of its sum_, the entry's example. The
    Wagner sum's ln `p_r` is taken each call, about a tenth of a call after the shared logarithm;
    holding it would need a literal pinned to `math::ln`'s bits in every row's equation (`libm`'s
    `log` is not `const`), a change to the registry's rows. decision-r09-climate-levers §7 names
    the batch's levers: B4, the shared logarithm and B3 with B9. For the performance list.
  - _The tests._ `PowBase`'s six tests take 2.6 s natively, 2.5 s on wasip1 and 4.2 s in V8 (the
    `slow-test` profile); the diagram's oracle test 0.15 s; B3 and B9's 6.3 s. B4's oracle checks
    ride in `the_small_earth_s_mean_and_lakes_hold_after_the_whole_pass` (after the climate, read
    as a one-month year, and after the step), which already passes the small Earth through the
    erosion, so they take no climate pass of their own (that test 109.8 s natively).
  - _Acceptance, as built._ The entry's, with `cargo test -p hyperion-base math::pow_base`

    `cargo test -p hyperion-sim --lib substance::diagram`, which hold `PowBase` and the diagram's
