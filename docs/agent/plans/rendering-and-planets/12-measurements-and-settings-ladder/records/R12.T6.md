# R12.T6: records as built

The as-built records of R12.T6, moved from the Risks and open points of
[12-measurements-and-settings-ladder.md](../../12-measurements-and-settings-ladder.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **R12.T6, as built.** Records compare when their scene and version, machine identity (its
  hardware and display mode), power profile, `setting.quality`, display kind and
  `harness.gpuTiming` agree (a control with a control); software versions and `setting.values`
  may differ and are printed as the change, so T11's before and after records compare. A verdict
  needs C(n + m, n) ≥ 252, five against five's p = 2/252, else `SAME`. `just perf-compare <id>
[--against <id>]` exits 0 on any verdict, 1 on a refusal or an unknown ID; `perfCompare` is the
  pure function behind it.
