# R12.T5.a: records as built

The as-built records of R12.T5.a, moved from the Risks and open points of
[12-measurements-and-settings-ladder.md](../../12-measurements-and-settings-ladder.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **R12.T5.a, as built.** `view/perf/scenes.ts` (`PERF_SCENES`, `PerfScene`) and
  `view/perf/scripts.ts` (`PerfScript`; `ringedGiantApproach`, `arrivalHold`, `nuclearDiscSweep`).
- **Shared with the main process.** The pins, each scene's arrival (`PerfPlacement`), server and
  height workers are `@hyperion/perf`'s `PERF_PINS` and `PERF_SCENE_HARNESS`.
- **Views** come from R07's `viewBudgets`. An instrument is 240 × 180 px, R07's 15 × 11.25 rem
  canvas.
- **R12.T5.a, leans (2026-11-17).** The approach ends 100 m above the ring plane at the annulus's
  middle radius, from 20° above it, looking at the giant.
- **The sweep is 300 au (lean, 2026-11-17)**, from −150 au to +150 au along galactic x, 1 au above
  the barycentre, turning once about z: each re-bake needs about 30 au (R06's 0.1 px rule).
- **Workers.** Height workers are the machine's default but three on the station and the arrival.
  Servers run `--num-workers 2`, the station's co-resident one 1.
- **One `PerfCriterion` a setting**, so the station's 1 GB memory ceiling is not carried: its
  criterion is the frame column alone.
- **The scenes' views.** The approach and the sweep draw a photorealistic primary alone, and
  `arrival-memory` the cockpit's views.
- **The approach's frame as placed (lean, 2026-11-18).** Its `body-fixed` script stands in the
  giant's widest ring's frame: z its normal on the host star's side, x toward the star in its plane,
  about the giant's non-rotating frame (`scriptedCamera.ts`).
- **`arrivalHold`** gives the spike its two poses; `arrivalHolds.ts` times the waits.
