# R12.T4.b: records as built

The as-built records of R12.T4.b, moved from the Risks and open points of
[12-measurements-and-settings-ladder.md](../../12-measurements-and-settings-ladder.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **R12.T4.b, as built.** `main/perf/`: `runner.ts` (`PerfRunner`: `prepare`, `operations`,
  `fault`), `server.ts` (`LocalServer`), `steadiness.ts` (`judgeWarmUp`, `trendOf`), `assemble.ts`
  (`assemblePerfRecord`), `memory.ts` (the 1 Hz `PerfMemorySampler`), `frameTrace.ts`, `handlers.ts`
  and `session.ts`; the preload's `perfApi.ts` and `perfLaunch.ts`; `scripts/perfRun.sh` behind
  `just perf-run`. The generator version is `hyperion-base`'s `GENERATOR_VERSION`.
- **The port, settled (lean, 2026-11-17).** `--port 0` and the server's `listening addr=` line.
- **The preload's calls beyond Design note 14 (lean, 2026-11-17).** `describe(description)` comes
  before the warm-up, with the scene, setting values, views and adapter. `config()` and
  `beginRepetition(n)` also give the arrival: the main process runs `scripts/placeShip.mjs` at the
  pins, the repetitions' inside the span. `finish(end)` takes `complete` with notes or `failed`
  with a reason.
- **R12.T4.b, as built (2).** A run that fails before its first repetition ends leaves no record,
  since a record holds one. The warm-up also judges the renderer's median frame interval within 5%
  (lean). The trend flag goes in `notes`. Every repetition has a `compositing` row, `null` where
  the busy time is unread. The trace is cycled every 120 s and reduced after the span. The machine
  slug comes from the PCI IDs (`machineSlugOf`). The software mode takes `--perf-run`.
