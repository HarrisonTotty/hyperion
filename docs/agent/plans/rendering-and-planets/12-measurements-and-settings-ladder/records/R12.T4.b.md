# R12.T4.b: records as built

The as-built records of R12.T4.b, moved from the Risks and open points of
[12-measurements-and-settings-ladder.md](../../12-measurements-and-settings-ladder.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **R12.T4.b, as built.** `main/perf/`: `runner.ts` (`PerfRunner`: `prepare`, `operations`,
  `fault`), `server.ts` (`LocalServer`), `steadiness.ts` (`judgeWarmUp`, `trendOf`) and
  `assemble.ts` (`assemblePerfRecord`).
- **The rest of `main/perf/`.** `memory.ts` (the 1 Hz `PerfMemorySampler`), `frameTrace.ts`,
  `handlers.ts` and `session.ts`; the preload's `perfApi.ts` and `perfLaunch.ts`;
  `scripts/perfRun.sh` behind `just perf-run`.
- **The generator version** is `hyperion-base`'s `GENERATOR_VERSION`.
- **The port, settled (lean, 2026-11-17).** `--port 0` and the server's `listening addr=` line.
- **The preload's calls beyond Design note 14 (lean, 2026-11-17).** `describe(description)` comes
  before the warm-up, with the scene, setting values, views and adapter.
- **The arrival.** `config()` and `beginRepetition(n)` also give the arrival: the main process runs
  `scripts/placeShip.mjs` at the pins, the repetitions' inside the span.
- **The end.** `finish(end)` takes `complete` with notes or `failed` with a reason.
- **R12.T4.b, as built (2).** A run that fails before its first repetition ends leaves no record,
  since a record holds one. The warm-up also judges the renderer's median frame interval within 5%
  (lean).
- **Notes and rows.** The trend flag goes in `notes`. Every repetition has a `compositing` row,
  `null` where the busy time is unread.
- **The trace, the slug, the software mode.** The trace is cycled every 120 s and reduced after the
  span. The machine slug comes from the PCI IDs (`machineSlugOf`), `swiftshader` in the software
  mode, which takes `--perf-run`.
- **The switches in force (Design note 5).** The record's `harness.switches` are those in force: the
  recipe's on the command line before the entry script (`perfSwitchesInForce`), then perf mode's.
- **The recipe's switches, included.** With `--software`, test-render's: SwiftShader's,
  `--enable-unsafe-webgpu` among them. `--user-data-dir` is left out; `--ozone-platform=` gives the
  window system.
- **A repetition that cannot begin** (the restart's `ServerError`, the trace not started, the
  placement refused) ends the run with a record of those done, and `beginRepetition` is refused.
- **The run's log.** The renderer's console warnings and errors go to it (`perf run: renderer: `),
  with a line at `config()` and at `describe()`, so that a stalled run is placed.
- **`@hyperion/perf` is bundled** into the main process and the preload (`externalizeDeps.exclude`):
  a sandboxed preload can require no package (`preload/bundledImports.test.ts`).
