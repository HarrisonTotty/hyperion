# R12.T3.b: records as built

The as-built records of R12.T3.b, moved from the Risks and open points of
[12-measurements-and-settings-ladder.md](../../12-measurements-and-settings-ladder.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **Platform audit, memory (lean, 2026-11-13).** Off Linux the headline is `nvidia-smi`'s device
  memory less its baseline (WDDM's per-process N/A reads `null`), else `adapter-tally`. A tally is
  a lower bound on resident bytes: one above the ceiling fails, one below it reads `NOT JUDGED`, as a
  tally. `workingSetSize` differs by platform, and a comparison never crosses platforms (the
  machine's identity holds it).

- **R12.T3.b, as built.** `samplers.ts` adds `sampleGpuMemory(pid, drivers, sources, atMs)`
  (`drmDriversOf` picks the GPU's drivers, so another driver's fdinfo is skipped),
  `sampleDisplayServer` with `findDisplayServerPid` (Xorg by `/proc/<pid>/comm`), `sampleNvidia`,
  `nvidiaBaseline`, `appMemoryOf`, `summariseDrm`, `summariseNvidia` and `gpuMemoryHeadline`.
  `summariseDrm` leaves out the samples within 2 s of a tally release of 16 MiB or more (lean,
  2026-11-17). R05's `DrmClient` adds `sharedBytes` and `engineNs`; `readDrmClients` is split out.
- **R12.T3.b, as built (2).** Each height worker's message carries `wasmBytes`
  (`HeightWorkerMessage`); the pool's `workerMemory`, and the session's getter of that name, list
  each running worker's field copy and largest WebAssembly memory for the record's arrays. The
  tally's releases (`TallyRelease`) reach the main process over R12.T4.b's preload, not built here.
- **R12.T3.b, pending the owner.** The fdinfo fixtures are written from `drm-usage-stats.rst` and
  Design note 6's probe; the captures and the by-hand acceptance (a non-zero resident figure from
  `nvidia-smi` on the desktop, from fdinfo with Xorg's line on the UHD 620) are NOT RUN in a pod.
- **R12.T3.b, as built (3).** `sampleDisplayServer(pid, drivers, sources, atMs)` and
  `sampleNvidia(gpuPid, baselineBytes, sources, atMs)` take the runner's GPU process, drivers and
  baseline. Resident, total and shared sum every `drm-*-<region>`, as R05's `sumDrmClients` does
  (`stolen-system0` is 0 for the GPU process); `appMemoryOf` groups `workingSetSize` by type.
- **R12.T3.b, left to R12.T4.c.** On NVIDIA `busyNs` is `null`, so the record's assembly and
  `table.ts` must print the `compositing` row as `NOT AVAILABLE` (hardware item 2), not `—`;
  nothing here prints it.
- **R12.T3.b, the laptop's capture (2026-11-18).** The fdinfo fixtures are now captured on the
  UHD 620, five minutes into a low descent.
  - The GPU process held six i915 clients on nine descriptors, client 17 behind four. Two of them
    were resident: 61,008 KiB with 14,696 KiB shared, and 186,168 KiB.
  - Xorg's client 4 sat behind five descriptors, with 23,024 KiB resident. It is readable because
    Xorg runs as the user.
  - The acceptance's UHD 620 half, a non-zero resident figure from fdinfo with Xorg's line, is met
    as raw fdinfo. R12's sampler itself has not yet run on the laptop; that needs a `perf-run`.
  - The desktop's `nvidia-smi` half stays the owner's.
