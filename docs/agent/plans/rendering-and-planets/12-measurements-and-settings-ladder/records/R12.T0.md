# R12.T0: records as built

The as-built records of R12.T0, moved from the Risks and open points of
[12-measurements-and-settings-ladder.md](../../12-measurements-and-settings-ladder.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **The ladder inventory** is written by T0, below.
- **Consumes resolved by `grep` (lean, 2026-11-13).** Every name T1–T7 call resolves but four,
  each with its owner: `percentiles.ts` (R05, none by its ruling: `nearestRank` and `frameStats` in
  `main/results.ts` serve); `TerrainPass.gpuBytes` (R05/R10: `TerrainSession.memory().gpuBytes`
  stands in); an EV-range type (R02: T4.c reads the view's exposure state); and R11's pass labels
  (`decoration`, `rocks`, `scatter`, `cloudShadow`, `still`) and categories (`clouds`, `ocean`,
  `rings`, `scatter`, `stills`), unbuilt, the PassList's `rings`, `clouds` and `ocean` slots
  `built: false`.
- **Consumes moved or grown.** `ViewLabelBlock` is under `renderer/src/displays/view/`;
  `TABLE_SIZES` is `view/atmosphere/hillaire.ts`'s; `GpuCapabilities` adds `depthClipControl`,
  `primitiveIndex` and `subpixelBits`; `MemoryCategory` adds R06's `sky-cube` and `sky-scratch` and
  R14's `geometry-clusters` and `geometry-pages`, which T3.a maps (to `other` unless it adds a
  row). `ViewSlot` is `primary | instrument` (`budget/viewBudget.ts`): a record's `station` role is
  a station machine's wireframe `primary` (T4.c).
- **The descent's span, as built:** 1,230 s (`DESCENT_SEGMENTS`, asserted by
  `descentProfile.test.ts`): orbit coast 60, descent arc 900, approach and flare 120, low fast pass
  30, slowdown 60, vertical descent 10, hover and touchdown 50 s. Design note 13 holds.
- **Ladder inventory: R05's `SETTINGS`** (`view/quality/qualitySetting.ts`, tests
  `qualitySetting.test.ts`; high / low). Terrain detail: `tauPx` 1 / 2 (R05 DN 7), `normals`
  double / mesh (DN 4), `vertexPath` baked-offsets / face-differences (to R05.T18), `cacheBytes`
  400 MB / 64 MiB (R10.T14.b, `cacheBudget.test`), `handoverDetail` faded / whole (R10.T15.b.2).
  Resolution: `renderHeightPx` 1,080 / 720 (DN 26), `internalScaleBounds` [0.5, 1] on both (R07 DN
  14), `budget` 60 / 30 Hz and views null / 1 (R07.T18, `viewBudget.test`), `photoreal`'s
  `histogramStride` 1 / 2 (R07.T12) and bloom 7 / 5 levels (R07.T14, `bloom.test`). Beyond the
  ladder: `discAnnuli` 4 / 3, `planetshineSources` 2 / 1, `floorLimbSamples` 2 / 2 (R07.T17,
  T8.i).
- **Ladder inventory: the atmosphere.** `TABLE_SIZES` (`hillaire.test` and others): sky view
  192 × 108 / 128 × 64 at 75 / 16 steps, aerial perspective 32³ / 32² × 16, ray march at scale 1 /
  0.5 and 36 / 16 samples (R05.T12.c, T12.e, R08.T7); `aerialPerspectiveScope` scene / terrain
  (R08.T9.a); `marchBand` and `marchDepthSteps` (R08.T8, T8.e, T11.b). R08's `settings.ts`
  (`settings.test`): `ATMOSPHERE_VIEW_SETTINGS` (sky suns 2 / 2, planetshine 2 / 1,
  `qualityLimits` for T7.b, `giantRoute`; R08.T7, T16.a.2); `ATMOSPHERE_SETTINGS`' levers are equal
  on both settings, beyond the ladder.
- **Ladder inventory: R11, the sky, R14 and the rest.** R11.T1's fields (`decorationSetting.ts`,
  `decorationSetting.test`): `decoration` (terrain detail), `scatterDensity` 1 / 0 (scatter),
  `clouds` volume / layer and `cloudShadows` (clouds, shadows), `oceanComponents` 32 / 8,
  `oceanRefraction`, `whitecaps` (ocean), `rings` particles / annulus and `ringShadowOnBody`
  (rings). The sky's `HIGH_SKY` / `LOW_SKY` (`view/sky/setting.ts`, R06.T13.f): faces 3,072 /
  1,024 px, sprites 4,096 / 2,048, `nMax` 300,000 / 100,000. R14's `GeometrySettings`
  (`qualitySetting.ts`, `geometryLaunch.test`, `spikeSession.test`): `pageBytes` 128 / 32 MiB and
  `meshTolerancePx` 1 / 2 (terrain detail), the rest equal on both or levers (R14.T1–T6). Also
  `STILL_TILE_MAX_PX` 4,096 / 2,048 (R11 DN 17) and `TERRAIN_TRIANGLE_BUDGET` (R14.T3.e, no test
  names it). The cascades have no field: `setting === "high"` in `terrainPass.ts`, `session.ts`
  and `materialSet.ts`.
- **Timed pass labels** (`onPassTimes`; roles `primary` and `instrument`). Photorealistic: `sky`,
  `disc cells`, `terrain shadows` (high), `bodies` (terrain draws inside it), `discs`,
  `atmosphere tables`, `atmosphere view`, `atmosphere behind`, `atmosphere composite`, `histogram`,
  `bloom`, `tonemap`, `symbology`; off on both settings, `figure id raster`, `figure resolve`,
  `terrain id raster`, `terrain id raster late`, `terrain resolve`, `depth pyramid`, `bodies late`.
  Wireframe: `view:wireframe`, `hull cluster cull`, `mesh occluder cull`. Both: `terrain cluster
cull`, `terrain cluster compact`, `body cluster cull`, and at a bake `sky bake` and
  `sky:bakeSplat`. R05's spike: `terrain`, `spike display`. `terrain clusters (<viewId>) …` carry
  the view's ID, so T4.c keys them by their prefix.
- **The platform audit, settled (lean, 2026-11-13).** R07's quiet rules already take `quietOf`
  (`viewsCheckResults.ts`, `smoke/childWindow.ts`); `x11RelaunchArgs`, macOS's window lifecycle
  and a missing `nvidia-smi` stay as they are. T2's, T3.b's and T4.a's items are in their records.
- **The inventory's benchmarks** are not listed (lean, 2026-11-13): T8.a reads each owning plan's
  recorded benchmarks from its records when it enters them as `plan-benchmark` records.
