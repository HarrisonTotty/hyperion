# R12.T4.c: records as built

The as-built records of R12.T4.c, moved from the Risks and open points of
[12-measurements-and-settings-ladder.md](../../12-measurements-and-settings-ladder.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **R12.T4.c, as built.** `PASS_ROWS` has T0's labels and R11's `decoration` and `clouds`;
  `PASS_ROW_PREFIXES` keys `terrain clusters (<view ID>) …`.
- **The files.** `view/perf/`: `passRows.ts`, `repetitionMetrics.ts`, `runPerfScene.ts`
  (`PerfSceneRun`), `descentDriver.ts`, `PerfApp.tsx`.
- **The leans (lean, 2026-11-17).** A resolve's role comes from its labels. The engine is kept
  across remounts. Rows count frames whose every resolve reported.
- **The descent's measurement (lean, 2026-11-17).** The descent is measured at the warm-up; later
  mounts fly at once, the field arriving in the span.
- **Fixed values (lean, 2026-11-17).** Internal scale 1 (the spike draws unscaled); `kernelPaths`,
  `arrival`, `heightPointUs` `null`.
- **The views are recorded as drawn**: each canvas's size as the spike drew it, from the first drawn
  frame (`PerfDrawn.views`).
- **The station draws its wireframe alone (research-leans-54, ruling 7).** At 1920 × 1080, its
  canvas's own size (`DescentSpikePerf.mainSizePx`), the instruments off; the scene is version 2.
- **The station's window is 1080p on both settings** (Design note 8's station, `perfWindowSize`);
  every other scene's is 1080p at high and 720p at low.
- **The screen after the run.** `PERFORMANCE RUN COMPLETE`, or on a failure the spike's own fault
  line where it showed one, else `PERFORMANCE RUN FAILED: <cause>` in the guide's voice.
- **The other three scenes (2026-11-18).** `arrival-memory` on the spike's driver with
  `arrivalHolds.ts`; `ringed-giant-approach` and `nuclear-disc-sky` on `VIEW`'s free scripted camera
  (`viewDriver.ts`, `PerfViewStage.tsx`, `scriptedCamera.ts`; `ViewDisplay`'s `perf` prop,
  `displays/view/viewPerf.ts`). `driverFor.ts`'s `perfDriverFor` replaces `descentDriverFor`.
- **A measured span.** `PerfDrawn.span` is `script` or `measured`; a measured one ends at
  `PerfSceneSink.spanEnded`, its segments as run (`arrival`, `deepest`, `held`).
- **The holds (lean, 2026-11-18).** The field is resident once its answer is held, every block
  reported and every worker's copy equal; the deepest pose's patches once it draws patches for 2 s
  with no `TERRAIN: STREAMING` and no patch made resident, or 120 s after the jump.
- **`VIEW` on a perf run (lean, 2026-11-18).** `MAXIMIZE VIEW` on at mount, its label block whole
  (the guide's row says off); `CAMERA` reads `SCRIPTED`, at 60°; the camera's controls are not held
  back; the wireframe stand-in's frames are left out; its internal scale is measured.
- **Two rows added.** `primary:hull cluster cull` and `primary:mesh occluder cull` are `other`:
  `VIEW`'s photorealistic symbology culls its own ship's hull through R02's wireframe list.
- **`VIEW`'s universe (lean, 2026-11-18).** Taken from the server's list, never opened: an opening
  warms the galaxy's sky and tube tables on the scene's two workers. An unanswered scene is asked
  again up to three times.
- **Left undone.** The arrival record (the field's and sky's transfer bytes and times) and every
  view's exposure stay `null`; R11's rings have no photorealistic pass, so no rings row is lit.
- **By hand.** A pod's SwiftShader run of one `cockpit-descent` repetition gave a record that
  `just perf-record` accepts (2026-11-18); the development machine's run is pending.
