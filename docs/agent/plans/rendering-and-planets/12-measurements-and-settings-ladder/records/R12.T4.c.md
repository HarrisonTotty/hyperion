# R12.T4.c: records as built

The as-built records of R12.T4.c, moved from the Risks and open points of
[12-measurements-and-settings-ladder.md](../../12-measurements-and-settings-ladder.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **R12.T4.c, as built.** `PASS_ROWS` has T0's labels and R11's `decoration` and `clouds`;
  `PASS_ROW_PREFIXES` keys `terrain clusters (<view ID>) …`.
- **The files.** `view/perf/`: `passRows.ts`, `repetitionMetrics.ts`, `runPerfScene.ts`
  (`PerfSceneRun`), `descentDriver.ts`, `PerfApp.tsx`.
- **The leans (lean, 2026-11-17).** A resolve's role comes from its labels. The engine is kept
  across remounts. Rows count frames whose every resolve reported.
- **The descent's measurement (lean, 2026-11-17).** The descent is measured at the warm-up; later
  mounts fly at once, the field arriving in the span.
- **Fixed values (lean, 2026-11-17).** Internal scale 1 (the spike draws unscaled); `kernelPaths`,
  `arrival`, `heightPointUs` `null`.
- **The views are recorded as drawn**: each canvas's size as the spike drew it, from the first drawn
  frame (`PerfDrawn.views`).
- **The station keeps the spike's two instruments** (pending a ruling): they draw beside the
  station's view, their passes counted in `station-wireframe`, since a resolve names no view.
- **The station's window is 1080p on both settings** (Design note 8's station, `perfWindowSize`);
  every other scene's is 1080p at high and 720p at low.
- **The screen after the run.** `PERFORMANCE RUN COMPLETE`, or on a failure the spike's own fault
  line where it showed one, else `PERFORMANCE RUN FAILED: <cause>` in the guide's voice.
- **Left undone.** `ringed-giant-approach`, `arrival-memory` and `nuclear-disc-sky` have no renderer
  driver and end at once, failed.
- **By hand.** A pod's SwiftShader run of one `cockpit-descent` repetition gave a record that
  `just perf-record` accepts (2026-11-18); the development machine's run is pending.
