The as-built records of R11.T10.e, linked from its entry under Tasks in
[11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md](../../11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md).

- **The still's terrain view (ruling 4a), as built (2026-11-18).**
  - `stills/stillTerrain.ts`: `STILL_TERRAIN_VIEW_ID` (`STILL`), `StillTerrainSession` (the
    session's seams a still takes), `StillTerrainCapture` and `StillTerrainView`, made with the
    still's renderer: it notes the still's view at its own size, `streamPriority: "bulk"`, holds the
    survey, gives each sample a `FrameTerrain` at the time it is drawn, and forgets the view at its
    disposal.
  - Terrain: `TerrainViewReport.streamPriority` takes `"bulk"`, at `BULK_VIEW_WEIGHT` (0.25, lean).
    `ViewSelectionInput.bulk`, `TerrainPassView.bulk` and `PatchRequest.bulk`: a request only bulk
    views want orders after every other (`compareRequests`, the pool's `before`, `joinDemand`).
  - `TerrainPass.streamedFor(id)` and `TerrainSession.streamedFor(view, bodies)`: nothing missing or
    stood in, settled, no refinement asked, bands at τ′.
  - `TerrainSession.holdSurvey(bodies)` (lean, 2026-11-18): the cells are frozen by holding the
    store's later blocks and coverage back from the workers, masks and survey for every view while a
    still holds them, since each bake carries the workers' live survey. The live view's survey pauses
    for the still's length.
  - `PhotorealStillView.terrain?()`; `STILL_UNAVAILABLE.terrain` now holds only for a view handing a
    body over that gives no terrain; `DrawnPhotorealFrame.occluderSlopePx`.
  - Smoke part 28: `smoke/stillTerrain.ts`' `checkTerrainStill`, over a lit pass of the Moon-like
    fixture standing in for the session.
- **R11.T10.e.1, the size, as built (2026-11-18).**
  - `stills/stillSize.ts`: `STILL_SCALES` (1 to 4 times the view's output size, lean), `stillSizeAt`
    and `stillSizeOptions`. `StillControlState.size` (`StillSizeChoice`), `StillControlProps.onSize`
    and the `SIZE` row; `StillSession.setScale(scale)`, the largest offered at most it.
  - `StillSource.outputSize?()` and `snapshot(ask?: StillAsk)`: the output size is the view's
    presented size, its canvas's device px, whatever its internal scale or the setting's rows.
  - `DrawnPhotorealFrame.remake?(StillFrameAsk)`: `ViewFrameDrawer` makes the frame again from its
    `PhotorealInputs` at the request, the view's `HostDiscLayer` framed at once at the still's
    viewport and its sprites by `spritesAtScale`; the hand-over is measured at the view's own size.
  - Until T10.e.2 a still larger than the tile limit is drawn whole, the tiles the read-back's.
- **R11.T10.e.2, the tiles, as built (2026-11-18).**
  - R01: `FrameSubmission.tileOriginPx` and `frame.wgsl`'s `tile : vec4f` after the viewport
    (`FRAME_BYTES` 160; `DiscCellFrame.tileOriginPx`, a 40-float block); 0 for every view's frame.
  - Shaders: `bodyDisc.wgsl`'s `view_ray` takes the image's px less the tile's origin and
    `clipProjection`'s third column, and `image_pixel_centre`; `bodyDiscDraw.wgsl`,
    `starSprite.wgsl` and `sky/shaders/disc.wgsl` place the CPU's pixels less the origin. Also, a
    deviation from the brief's list, without which a tile's sky is drawn off its place:
    `smoothMesh.wgsl`'s and `figureResolve.wgsl`'s pixel (one line each), and `sky/shaders/band.wgsl`'s
    and `cube.wgsl`'s rays (M's off-axis terms). Digests re-pinned (`just pin-digests`): every
    shader composes `frame.wgsl`.
  - `PhotorealFrame.tile` (`sizePx`, `originPx`): every pass takes the origin, a tile draws no
    two-pass occlusion. `TerrainFrameInput.tile` and `LiveFrameTerrainInput.tile`: the lit pass's
    cluster cull takes `tileFrustum`.
  - `PhotorealStillRenderer`: units `scene <tile>` sample by sample; every tile drawn at the tiles'
    largest texture through `tileProjection` from its origin moved by the jitter, its rect added to
    the assembled image by `stills/stillAccumulate.ts`' `STILL_TILE_FIRST_MATERIAL` and
    `STILL_TILE_ADD_MATERIAL` (`stillAccumulate.wgsl`, in the catalogue), which replace
    `STILL_ACCUMULATE_MATERIAL`.
  - A frame drawing air is drawn whole, one tile of the still's size (lean, 2026-11-18): R08's passes
    rebuild their rays from their own camera, with no off-axis projection.
