# R11.T4.a: records as built

The as-built records of R11.T4.a, moved from the Risks and open points of
[11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md](../../11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **R11.T4.a, as built (2026-11-18).**
  - R10's hook is `BakeHook::bake(&self, patch: &BakedHeights<'_>, out) -> bool`: it reads the
    patch's own `f64` heights (`BakedHeights::own_sample`, `None` where a vertex took the reference
    surface) and answers whether its layer is complete; `false` leaves the bake not complete.
  - The lever: `HeightSource::vertex_height(cache, key, x, y, dir)`, `height`'s by default, through
    which the collision interpolant reads its vertices; the rock hook answers the patch's own there.
  - A finest patch's rocks are those whose axis it holds, `rocks_near`'s order (lean, 2026-11-18):
    each rock is drawn once, by one patch. `ExtraLayer::Rocks(Vec<Rock>)`, `Rock::frame()`,
    `rock_words` (`ROCK_WORDS` 20, the axis's direction last), `WorkerFields::{rocks_near,
surface_height_with_rocks}`; wasm `BakedTerrain.rocks()`, `SurfaceFields.rocksNear`,
    `surfaceHeightWithRocksM`, `rockWords()`.
  - Client: `TerrainLayers.rocks?: RockLayer`, `TerrainPass.rocks` (`ResidentRocks`, kept and
    forgotten with each bake). `rockMesh.ts`' `chordalBound` is the triangles' sampled radial depth
    × `CHORDAL_SAFETY` 1.25, held to a dense sampling over 10³ random rocks (lean, 2026-11-18).
  - Review fixes (2026-11-18): `patch_rocks_at` answers the first query the view could not
    answer; `CellAbundance { Site, Stated(k) }`; `scatter/bake_moon_like`, a new golden of the
    pinned finest patches' rock layers in `rock_words`, at 33. The worker-against-main-thread test
    runs the worker's own `answerRequest` in the test's thread, not in a `Worker`.
