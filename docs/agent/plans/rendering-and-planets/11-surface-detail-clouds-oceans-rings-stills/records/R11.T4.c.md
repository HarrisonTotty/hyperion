# R11.T4.c: records as built

The as-built records of R11.T4.c, moved from the Risks and open points of
[11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md](../../11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **R11.T4.c, as built (2026-11-18).**
  - Rust: `scatter/golden.rs`' `collision_{moon,earth,mars,ceres}_like`, 2,500 points a world
    (10⁴ in all), `<p> <ground_at> <surface_height_with_rocks>` a line, over the finest patches
    of the twelve pinned site cells: each rock's axis, two points inside its outline and one past
    it, then points drawn over the patches. Native, wasip1 and the browser's embedded arm; new
    goldens at 33, no bump, blessed on x86_64.
  - Vitest (`rockCollision.wasm.test.ts`): the four sampled worlds are the crate's own fields, which
    the client has no payload of, so the points are drawn by the same rule over the middle 3 × 3 of
    a 5 × 5 finest block of the worker fixtures (`moon_like`, `earth_like`), against the module's
    `surfaceHeightWithRocksM` there (lean, 2026-11-18). The drawn surface: the `baked-offsets`
    bake's `f32` q₀ on R05's diagonal, and the builder's rock pages, `f32` grid × step.
  - Bounds: bare ground within 3 mm (R10.T15.a's); on a rock, 3 mm above to (tolerance + half the
    grid's diagonal) ÷ cos(slope) + `contact_bound` + 3 mm below; steeper than cos 0.25 counted,
    not compared. Found: bare within 0.4 µm, rocks 6 µm above to 2.6 mm below. A control, the
    terrain alone, fails most rock points. `test/workerFieldFixtures.ts` is the fixtures' parser,
    shared with T4.a's test.
  - The Vitest points: 400 drawn over the middle patches and four a rock, a world; on a rock the
    drawn may stand above the module's by the grid's half step over the slope's cosine and 3 mm.
