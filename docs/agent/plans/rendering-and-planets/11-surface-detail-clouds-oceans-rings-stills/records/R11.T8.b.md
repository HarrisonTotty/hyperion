# R11.T8.b: records as built

The as-built records of R11.T8.b, moved from the Risks and open points of
[11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md](../../11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **R11.T8.b, as built: the data (2026-11-13).**
  - `WorkerFields::ocean_map(body) -> Result<OceanMap, QueryBodyError>` (no seed: records alone)
    and wasm `oceanMap(system, body)`: each surveyed cell's `depthM`, `levelM` (water surface less
    sea level), `ice`, 24 wind code bytes, with `windSpeedsMps()`, `months`, `seasonEccentricity`.
  - Its own worker query, `{ kind: "ocean-map" }` and `HeightWorkerPool.oceanMap(body)`, not on
    `ClassMapAnswer` (lean, 2026-11-13: fix-jagged-biome-edges owns `classMap.ts`).
  - `field::month_blend_of(months, e, mean_anomaly) -> Option<MonthBlend>`, `month_blend`'s core
    bit for bit, and wasm `monthBlend`; the twin `ocean/monthBlend.ts` is held to `months.golden`
    and to the export.
  - `ocean/oceanMask.ts`: `oceanMaskOf(answer)`, `oceanCellKindOf(depthM, ice)` (open water: depth
    above 0 and ice ≤ `OPEN_WATER_MAX_ICE`, 127), `oceanCellKindAt`.
  - `ocean/oceanMap.ts`: `OceanMapStore(engine, pool)` with `want`, `season(body, meanAnomalyRad)`,
    `step(budgetMs)`, `oceanMapOf(body)`; one `rgba16float` 2D array a body (depth, level, wind
    east, north; 0 off open water) in the coverage mask's layout, under the new `MemoryCategory`
    `ocean`. Nothing creates the store yet: wave 2's pass wires it.
  - The month blend of the wind (lean, 2026-11-13): mean speeds linear, along the blended vectors'
    direction; re-packed when the months change or the weight moves 1 ÷ 32.
