# R11.T4.b: records as built

The as-built records of R11.T4.b, moved from the Risks and open points of
[11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md](../../11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **R11.T4.b, as built (2026-11-18).**
  - `rockSelection.ts`: `couldTouch(body)` is one disc, the footprint widened by
    `LARGEST_ROCK_REACH_M` (5.417 m); `rocksToDraw(drawSet, mask, finestLevel, meshes, region,
setting)` in place of the sketch's `(patches, bodies, setting, style)`: both styles take the
    setting's rule (lean, 2026-11-18). The pass's region is its step's grounded contacts, each
    widened so.
  - The draw (`TerrainPass.rocks`, `TerrainPassOptions.rocks: RockSource`,
    `TerrainSessionOptions.rocks`): `RockMeshes` (its pool root slots alone, `ROCK_POOL_SHARE` 0.5
    of `pageBytes`, `scatter`) and a `RockView` a view, two camera passes, τ =
    `meshTolerancePx.camera` and τ = 0 for the region; `TerrainViewFrame.rocks` its counts.
  - `rock.wgsl` lights a rock by `shade_terrain` at its place in its patch (the ground's class
    weights, maturity and horizon map) with its own normal; `rockWire.wgsl` is black, stroked in
    `--text` at the index contour's width; `rockPage.wgsl` decodes; `ROCK_BINDINGS` 25–28.
  - Not built (lean): rocks on the way back (no cluster path), rocks as casters into the cascades,
    rocks in the overlay's depth; by the visibility buffer they draw forward until R11.T4's T5.e.
  - Review fixes (2026-11-18): the low setting annunciates where a rock left out would subtend a
    pixel (`ROCK_DETAIL_PX` 1), not the mesh tolerance's 2 px. The rocks' pool takes every
    resident finest patch's mesh first come, on both settings; a region patch whose mesh it refuses
    raises `TERRAIN: DETAIL LIMITED` (`RockMeshes.isRefused`), and gets no precedence (a finding).
  - Not built, the owner's ruling pending: Design note 21's "on high, rocks sample their site
    entry's `Bedrock` set"; a rock is shaded by its ground's material alone (Design note 3).
  - Tests in the pass (`terrainPassCascades.test.ts`): the high setting's rocks, the low setting's
    region alone, a restore, and by the visibility buffer.
- **Landed over R11.T9 and R11.T10.e (2026-11-19).**
  - `TerrainPass.streamedFor(id)` also waits for the rock meshes of the finest patches the view sees
    to be built (`RockMeshes.isBuilding`, after a sync). A still's samples, which wait on it, so
    draw the same rocks; holdSurvey freezes their bakes with the ground's.
  - Smoke: the rocks' parts are 33 (the bake, the builder and the draw) and 34 (by the visibility
    buffer, R14.T5.e).
- **The pool holds the could-touch region first (`r11-rocks-2`, 2026-11-26).**
  - `RockMeshes.sync(rocks, region?: RockPoolRegion)`: `RockPoolRegion { discs, sphereOf(key) }`
    (`NO_ROCK_REGION` by default, first come); the pass builds it once a step (`#rockRegion`: its
    `couldTouch` discs and the patch cache's spheres) before the sync, `#rocksBuiltFor`'s too.
  - A region mesh with no room evicts meshes outside the region, farthest from its nearest disc
    first (lean, 2026-11-26: the region's distance, not a camera's, so one pool serves every view);
    a mesh outside it is refused while a refused region patch waits on the room. `regionRefused`
    now stands only where the region alone passes the pool.
  - A refused (or evicted) patch is rebuilt once room frees (a mesh let go with its patch) or the
    region comes to hold it, where the room then fits; no longer on every change of the layers.
  - Not built: the coarser chordal tolerance outside the region (no smoke part measures a
    descent's region refusals; a finding).
