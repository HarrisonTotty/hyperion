- **R11.T2.a, as built (2026-11-13; built 2026-11-02, rebased onto v31).**
  - `surface/decoration/decorationLattice.ts` is the reference (`decorationSeed`, `latticeHash`,
    `latticePlace`, `latticeCellM`, `decorationLevel`, `largestCellM`, `octaveFade`,
    `decorationPxPerRad`, `DECORATION_CLASSES`, `mixOf`, `rippleOf`, `scarShares`,
    `decorateTexel`, `perturbNormal`); `decoration.wgsl`'s `decorate(…)` and `decoration_normal`
    are its twin. `detailTextures.ts` is `detailTextures.wgsl`'s reference (`detail_triplanar`,
    `detail_biplanar` over a `DetailProjection` and a `texture_2d_array` of slopes, albedo and hue
    deviations, with a repeating sampler). `decorationTwin.ts` holds `DECORATION_WGSL`,
    `DETAIL_TEXTURES_WGSL` and the catalogued twin, which runs alone in test-render part 25.
  - T2.b owes per texel: the slot's key and `patchUv`, the face's (s, t) and each slot's class
    code; per body the seed, `decorationLevel(a, bandLimitM())` and `scarShares` (the header
    carries no crater parameters, so a worker query of `craters::cumulative_density` is needed);
    per patch the ripple from the month's 10 m wind (`ClimateCell.wind`, which reaches no client
    value yet; the slot record's two pad words are free).
  - The hash keys a point of the face's lattice, not the patch's cell, so patches of every level
    meet with no seam; a cube edge is one. Scars change the slope alone (open question 18).
    T10.a's test line is complete (`stillPlan.test.ts`, "a tile's decoration fade").
  - Leans (2026-11-02): the repetition hiding's turn and offsets are the body's, not each patch's,
    since a per-patch turn would cut the detail at every patch edge (against the brainstorm's
    Materials bullet; logged as a finding). Biplanar stays compiled for the owner's UHD 620 run;
    high takes triplanar.
