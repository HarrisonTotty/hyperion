# R11.T9.d: records as built

The as-built records of R11.T9.d, moved from the Risks and open points of
[11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md](../../11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **R11.T9.d, as built** (2026-11-18; lane r11-ring-slab).
  - _Names._ `rings/ringSplit.ts` (`instancedShare`, `handOverDistance`, `pixelAngleRad`,
    `instancedPerSquareMetre`, `instancedRadiusMinM`, `instancedRadiusAt`,
    `RING_PARTICLE_RADII_M`); `rings/ringField.ts` (`ringFieldOf`, `packRingFieldParams`,
    `fieldWindowPixelRad`, `fieldTauAt`, `profileSteps`, `sheetPlaceOf`, `beamThrough`,
    `erfcApprox`, the twin `cellParticles` and `fieldParticles`, `pcg`); `rings/ringParticles.ts`
    (`RingParticles` with `release()`, `RING_PARTICLE_PLACE_KERNEL`, `RING_PARTICLE_MATERIAL`,
    `particlesOn`, `fieldRingOf`, `expectedFieldParticles`, `fieldBudgetScale`,
    `lambertSpherePhase`, `particlePhaseRatio`); WGSL `ringParticles.wgsl` (the placement) and
    `ringParticleDraw.wgsl` (the material).
  - _Contracts._ `ViewRing.gravitationalParameterM3S2` (the planet's mass × G, `EARTH_GM_M3S2`
    derived from `EARTH_MASS_KG`); `PhotorealFrame.sceneTime`;
    `PhotorealRenderer.prepareRingParticles(setting)` (nothing made on a setting without particles)
    and `ringParticleDraws`; `RingSlabRecord.instancedPixelRad` (row 4's w), whose `instancedShare`
    the slab gives up. Passes `ring particles` (before `discs`), dispatches `ring particle place`
    and `ring particle cull`, all in `PASS_ROWS`' rings row. A still makes the particles' pipelines
    with the rings' before its first sample where its setting draws them (R11.T10.b's rule).
  - _The field (lean, 2026-11-18)._ Nine octaves of radius, 5 m down to 1 cm, each in cells of λ s_k
    (a cell holds 64 on average) within 2 s_k ÷ θ; a particle placed where 2s ≥ dθ at its own
    distance and its bounds meet the view, as the slab's share at its crossing's footprint; the
    profile's radial steps (at most 4 edges) followed, nothing off the annulus. Dusty rings are
    never instanced (`handOverDistance` 0, which `fieldRingOf` skips): their grains stay the slab's.
  - _Layer._ Heights Gaussian at T9.b's σ = H_eff ÷ √(2π) = 4.46 m for every massive ring, whatever
    its τ.
  - _Shear (lean)._ A sheet about r₀ turning at Ω₀ = √(GM ÷ r₀³), each row of cells rigid at the
    linearised rate Ω₀ (1 − 3x ÷ 2r₀) (Hill's shear, −(3/2) Ω₀ x against the turning frame), rows
    straight; r₀ and the reference time held within 50 km radially, a pop past it; prograde along
    n × r̂. The frame slides rather than turns about the camera: seen from a camera at rest a
    particle moves at v₀ − (3/2) Ω₀ x, not Kepler's v₀ − ½ Ω₀ x, but stands within |x y| ÷ r₀ of its
    arc's place (centimetres over the field). The test holds each row's drift in the turning frame
    to −(3/2) Ω₀ x at its middle and the field's slope dv/dx to 5% of it, from a camera that
    co-orbits.
  - _Budget (lean)._ 2²⁰ particles a view; the pixel widened (`fieldBudgetScale`, a ray estimate)
    where the view would fill more than 60%, the slab's share narrowed with it. The estimate takes
    the field's own τ where each ray meets the plane (its steps', so a gap's and the annulus's edges'
    too) and, within the layer's 3σ, both halves of the view against planes 3σ away (twice the
    column seen, a margin). It is 0.92–1.51 of the twin's count over the ring, beyond its
    edge, over a gap and at the mid-plane. The pixel is never narrower than
    `fieldWindowPixelRad(τ_ref)` either, which keeps every octave's window within 400 cells (it
    widens at τ ≳ 9.8 at 1080p).
  - _Memory._ 2²⁰ particles take 32 MiB of records and 16 MiB of lists: R14's `ParticlePath`, under
    `geometry-clusters`, not Design note 18's `rings`. They are released at a frame on a setting
    without particles.
  - _Light (lean), not through `surfaceLighting.wgsl` (T9.c's lean)._ Lambert spheres of their
    band's albedo × 0.85–1.15 under the brightest star, reweighted to the slab's power law by P(α)
    ÷ P_Lambert(α) at the view axis's phase angle (`particlePhaseRatio`, finite to α = π, where
    both vanish). The beam through the layer above a particle, exp(−τ Q(z_s ÷ σ) ÷ μ₀), rides the
    colour's alpha (erfc by Abramowitz and Stegun's 7.1.26). The planet's shadow is taken at the
    camera's foot. Opaque, depth written. No surge, no ring-shine, no diffuse light on the unlit
    face.
  - _The transmittance test_ takes 4 × 10⁴ rays, not 10⁴: the mean's own noise is about 0.75% at
    10⁴ (a ray is blocked with p ≈ 0.36 at μ 0.5) and 0.4% at 4 × 10⁴.
  - _Smoke._ Part 31 checks the placement against its twin (1% of tags, 2 cm, 10⁻⁴ of a radius,
    the colour word's albedo and beam to 1 in each byte), the slab with its share taken against its
    twin (the GPU's `ring_instanced_share`), and a frame on both settings. The captures
    `r11-t9d-rings-close-{high,low}` go with part 1's (six ring frames, not four).
  - _R14._ T5.d's path restore test is T9.d's (`ringParticles.test.ts`); R14's consumer table still
    reads "Not built" for T9.d, left to R14's re-validation.
  - _Not built._ The volumetric extinction about a camera within the layer (the slab stays a
    plane): at 1080p the instanced share is already about 0.84 at 100 m, so the sub-pixel remainder
    is the small term; the owner's ruling is asked. A field on more than one ring a view.
