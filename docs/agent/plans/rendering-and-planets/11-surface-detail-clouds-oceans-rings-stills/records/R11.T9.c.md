    occluder is some 10³ times the star (litBody.wgsl's `circle_overlap_area` loses every digit of
    f32 there), its angles by `preciseAtan2`; the slab no longer composes litBody.wgsl.
  - _Surge × f_e (lean)._ The 0.20–0.28° surge is the particles' own and multiplies P; f_e's peak,
    degrees wide, is the interparticle one (answers T9.b's "for T9.c").
