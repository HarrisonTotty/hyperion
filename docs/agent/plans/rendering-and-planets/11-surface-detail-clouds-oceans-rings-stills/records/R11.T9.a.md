# R11.T9.a: records as built

The as-built records of R11.T9.a, moved from the Risks and open points of
[11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md](../../11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **R11.T9.a, as built** (2026-11-13; lane r11-ring-shadows, the paused WIP of 2026-11-02 resumed).
  - _Names._ `rings/ringProfile.ts` (`ringProfile`, `RingShape`, `RingBand`, `regionOf`),
    `ringPhotometry.ts`, `ringShadows.ts` (`shadowingRing`, `ringShadowRecordOf`,
    `packRingShadowRows`, `ringShadowOnBody`; `planetShadowOnRing` for T9.c). `ViewRing` extends
    `RingShape`.
  - _The record._ The shadow reads 10 disc-record rows (308–317, at most 16 bands), not a texture:
    `DISC_ROWS` 308 → 318. Past 16 bands the 16th runs to the outer edge at the densest τ.
  - _Carried by_ `LitBodyInput.ring` and `DiscRecord.ring`, set by `photorealFrame.ts`'
    `withRingShadows`. No `rings` `MemoryCategory` yet: T9.a allocates nothing.
  - _The WGSL._ `ringShadow.wgsl` replaces `litBody.wgsl`'s stub; its includer defines
    `ring_shadow_row` and `ring_shadow_axes` and composes `surfaceLighting.wgsl` first (`LIT_PROBE`
    9 is `--lit-probe no-ring-shadow`).
  - _Not "not called" on low._ Off, a record carries no ring rows, and `ring_shadow_on_body` returns
    1 after one row read a light (Design note 14 says it is not called).
  - _Shed with the floor (lean, 2026-11-13)._ `PhotorealInputs.floorStepped`, from
    `BudgetedScale.airStepped`, turns it off, as `decorationAllowed` does; Design note 18 lists no
    such shed.
  - _Terrain (lean, 2026-11-13)._ `terrain_ring_shadow` stays 1, and the resolve frame's ring row
    of the 2026-11-13 ruling in the plan's Risks is not built: only giants carry rings, and they
    draw no terrain. Pending main's ruling on which task builds it.
  - _Gaps unlabelled (lean, 2026-11-13)._ The provisional gaps' widths and τ show in the shadow with
    no label; the UX review asks the owner whether a provisional-profile label is owed.
  - _Leans (2026-11-13)._ Surge amplitude 1 + 0.45 ÷ (1 + τ); ϖ₀ porous ice (0.56, 0.50, 0.40),
    rock (0.20, 0.18, 0.16), gaps × 0.6; sparse below τ 0.3; dusty weight 0.9, backward g 0.3.
  - _Not re-checked._ The dusty g₁ of 0.7 is still from memory: no source could be reached.
  - _Cost (lean, 2026-11-13)._ One `exp` a star a lit fragment of a ringed body: at most 0.1 ms.
  - _Pending: the owner's runs._ The Mac pair `ring-shadow-on-body` (a body pair, on the served
    giant 41ffecb1ffc00013.0400), and the RTX look at a Saturn-like ring's shadow on its planet.
