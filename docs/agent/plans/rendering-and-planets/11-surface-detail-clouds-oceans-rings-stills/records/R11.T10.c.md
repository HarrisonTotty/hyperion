# R11.T10.c: records as built

The as-built records of R11.T10.c, moved from the Risks and open points of
[11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md](../../11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **R11.T10.c, as built (2026-11-13).**
  - The API is `HyperionApi.stills: StillsApi` (`saveStill`, `listStills`, `readStill`), not three
    members of `HyperionApi`; channels `hyperion:stills:{save,list,read}`; answers
    `StillSaveAnswer`, `SavedStill[]` (newest first, at most 256), `StillFile | null`.
  - `StillLabel` is typed in `preload/api.ts`; `stills/stillLabel.ts` holds the schema
    (`parseStillLabel`, `stillLabelText`) and `stillLabel(request: StillRequest, streamed)`.
  - The label adds the camera's `orientation`, the `size`, each body's `name`, an inhibit's `cause`
    and `readings`, the view's label-block lines at the request, which `StillList` shows (the UX
    review). T10.b's source fills `StillRequest` from the view.
  - Main: `main/stills.ts`' `StillFolder` and `registerStillHandlers`; the window check is
    `ipcSender.ts`' new `isWindowsOwnPage`, and `pageOf` now parses URLs (the dev server's
    `http://localhost:5173` is its frame's `…/`, for every handler).
  - The two preloads share no module: a shared one became a chunk the sandboxed preload cannot
    `require`. Answers are read back in `stills/stillAnswers.ts` (`stillsOver`); the smoke's preload
    calls `smoke/result.ts`' `SMOKE_STILLS_CHANNELS`.
  - Bounds (lean, 2026-11-13): a side of 8,192 px (`STILL_MAX_SIDE_PX`); a PNG of
    H(1 + 4W) × 65/64 + 1 MiB (`stillPngLimitBytes`); a label of 64 KiB; one save at a time (`busy`).
  - `StillControlState` is `{ standing, activity, heldBack }`, `standing` `available`, `suspended`
    or `disabled` (`STILLS DISABLED`); the mount is `displays/view/ViewStills.tsx`, fold panel
    `stills` (`STILLS`).
  - Smoke part 28 (25 to 27 are other lanes'): the PNG round trip through the harness's main process
    and the mount. Left to T10.b: the job, its session and the mount's source.
