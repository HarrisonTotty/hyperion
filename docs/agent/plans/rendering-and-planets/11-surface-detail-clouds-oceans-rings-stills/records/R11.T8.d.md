# R11.T8.d: records as built

The as-built records of R11.T8.d, moved from the Risks and open points of
[11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md](../../11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **R11.T8.d, as built (2026-11-18).**
  - `ocean/shoreline.ts` (`setRmsHeightM`, `shoreAmplitudeScale`, `surfSimilarity`,
    `shoreFoamShare`, `shorePixelReachM`, `shoreFade`) and `shoreline.wgsl`, a library composed
    into `OCEAN_NEAR_WGSL`, with no catalogue entry of its own.
  - The waves' header is now (count, H_rms, L₀, 0). `packOceanWaves` takes `peakWavelengthM`, and
    `oceanNearPeakWavelengthM` and `OceanNearState.peakWavelengthM` are new. The near sea's alpha
    is now the depth fade, where it was 1.
  - Leans (2026-11-18): the broken share takes the offshore H_rms, and ξ takes H_rms, not H_m0.
    The foam falls linearly from ξ 0.5 to 3.3, and every component shares one amplitude scale (no
    shoaling or TMA).
  - Leans (2026-11-18): the fade spans max(5 cm, ℓ tan β), ℓ the pixel's reach along the slope.
    The shore foam is lit at the whitecaps' 0.22 and drawn only where the whitecaps are (high).
  - The bed's slope comes from three depth-copy pixels, so only within 10⁵ m (beyond, α 1). A vertex
    behind a nearer surface takes depth 0: the waves flatten for one grid cell along any edge in
    front of the sea.
  - A fix to T8.c: the grid's border is a skirt left undisplaced (`oceanGridMesh`'s z 1). From 2 m
    the waves had pulled the grid's edge into the view.
  - Smoke part 27: `checkOceanShore` (a plane-cast 1-in-20 beach, the fade against its twin at the
    sea's drawn depth, the surf's foam and heave) and `captureCoast` (bare beach at the frames'
    bottom).
- **R11.T8.d, by hand (2026-11-18).** `just test-render --part=27 --captures=<dir>`,
  `r11-t8d-coast-{1km,100m,2m}`: a 1-in-20 sand beach, a 7 m/s onshore wind, airless (black sky).
  - 1 km: a crisp, straight waterline with a thin teal band of shallows. The surf is under a pixel.
  - 100 m: blue water turns teal over the shallows, the waves visible. The waterline is soft, a few
    pixels of grey foam, with no hard edge or shimmer.
  - 2 m: the bed shows through teal water. The waves flatten towards a greyish surf band, and the
    sea reaches the frame's bottom edge.
  - The foam reads grey against the sand at 0.22. Fresh surf foam is 0.4 to 0.55: the owner's call
    (`findings.txt`).
