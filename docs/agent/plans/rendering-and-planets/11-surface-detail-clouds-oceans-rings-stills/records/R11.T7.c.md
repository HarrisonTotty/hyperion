# R11.T7.c: records as built

The as-built records of R11.T7.c, moved from the Risks and open points of
[11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md](../../11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **The kernels, as built (2026-11-14; nothing drawn yet).** Under `clouds/`:
  - `cloudVolume.ts`: `twoLobeOf(g)` (forward lobe at g + (1 − g) ÷ 2, backward at −0.3; lean),
    `twoLobePhase`, `CLOUD_OCTAVES` (4 octaves; extinction a 0.1102 on the sun's and the view's
    paths, scattering b = a, phase c 0), `marchColumn` (→ `{ single, multiple }`),
    `fluxReflectance`, `octaveReflectance`, `octaveCorrectionOf(optics)` and `octaveCorrectionAt`
    (K on the multiple scattering, 32 × 16 `r32float` over τ 0.05–10⁴ and μ₀ 0.02–1, 2 KiB a
    condensate), and the deck march's twin `marchDeckRay(clouds, deck, correction, ray)`.
  - `shaders/cloudVolume.wgsl`: `cloud_column_march`, `cloud_octave_correction_at` and
    `cloud_volume_march(o, d, sun, base_m, top_m, steps, footprint_per_m) -> vec2f` (radiance per
    unit irradiance, view transmittance); its includer binds `cloud_octave_correction`.
  - The probe's `column` and `deck` cases, held to their twins in smoke part 26.
- **The octaves add no light, and a correction meets δ-Eddington (lean, 2026-11-14).** Octaves
  with b > a (22–23% from δ-Eddington) added light without bound along a grazing view, so b ≤ a,
  one extinction scale on both paths; alone they miss δ-Eddington by 59%. The octaves past the
  first take K = (R_δE − R₀) ÷ R_multiple, so the corrected march meets δ-Eddington per column to
  3% (μ₀ ≥ 0.5, τ 1–50) and per coverage cell to 2%, R ≤ 1 over the table, and a grazing ray
  stays under 2 ÷ π.
- **Leans:** a column's τ is the field's mean τ read on the layer's shell above the sample, uniform
  through the deck; the sun's τ above a sample is the column's share over the local μ₀; a sample
  whose sun is below its horizon (μ < −√(2h ÷ a)) takes no sun.
