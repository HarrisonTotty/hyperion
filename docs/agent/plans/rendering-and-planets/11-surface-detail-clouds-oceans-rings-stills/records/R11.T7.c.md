# R11.T7.c: records as built

The as-built records of R11.T7.c, moved from the Risks and open points of
[11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md](../../11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **The kernels, as built (2026-11-14; nothing drawn yet).** Under `clouds/`:
  - `cloudVolume.ts`: `twoLobeOf(g)` (forward lobe at g + (1 − g) ÷ 2, backward at −0.3; lean),
    `twoLobePhase`, `CLOUD_OCTAVES` (4 octaves; extinction a 0.1102 on the sun's and the view's
    paths, scattering b = a, phase c 0), `marchColumn` (→ `{ single, multiple }`),
    `fluxReflectance`, `octaveReflectance`, `octaveCorrectionOf(optics)` and `octaveCorrectionAt`
    (K on the multiple scattering, 32 × 16 `r32float` over τ 0.05–10⁴ and μ₀ 0.02–1, 2 KiB a
    condensate), and the deck march's twin `marchDeckRay(clouds, deck, correction, ray)`.
  - `shaders/cloudVolume.wgsl`: `cloud_column_march`, `cloud_octave_correction_at` and
    `cloud_volume_march(o, d, sun, base_m, top_m, steps, footprint_per_m) -> vec2f` (radiance per
    unit irradiance, view transmittance); its includer binds `cloud_octave_correction`.
  - The probe's `column` and `deck` cases, held to their twins in smoke part 26.
- **The octaves add no light, and a correction meets δ-Eddington (lean, 2026-11-14).** Octaves
  with b > a (22–23% from δ-Eddington) added light without bound along a grazing view, so b ≤ a,
  one extinction scale on both paths; alone they miss δ-Eddington by 59%. The octaves past the
  first take K = (R_δE − R₀) ÷ R_multiple, so the corrected march meets δ-Eddington per column to
  3% (μ₀ ≥ 0.5, τ 1–50) and per coverage cell to 2%, R ≤ 1 over the table, and a grazing ray
  stays under 2 ÷ π.
- **Leans:** a column's τ is the field's mean τ read on the layer's shell above the sample, uniform
  through the deck; the sun's τ above a sample is the column's share over the local μ₀; a sample
  whose sun is below its horizon (μ < −√(2h ÷ a)) takes no sun.
- **Left to the wiring and later:** Design note 10's 128³, 32³ and 128² noises (the volume reads the
  layer's field alone); the quarter-resolution pass with temporal reprojection and its material;
  the transmittance map for its shadows; R08's transmittance to the deck (the caller's).
- **Pending: the owner's RTX 3080 run** of the volume's cost (1.5–3 ms, 10–20 MB).
- **The wiring, as built (2026-11-18).** `clouds/cloudVolumePass.ts` and three shaders.
  - A frame runs three passes on high: the `clouds volume` march at half the width and height, its
    steps jittered by the blue noise and the frame; the `clouds resolve`, which reprojects the
    layer shell's crossing into the last camera and clamps the history to the march's 3 × 3
    range; and the composite over the body's air as `clouds`.
  - It draws the nearest cloudy body alone, one history a view, and cuts that history at a cut, a
    new body, a new size, or a frame drawn without the volume.
  - The layer stands in until the condensate's correction is made (`OctaveCorrectionBuilder`, a row
    a frame, which is `octaveCorrectionOf` bit for bit), on low, and while the floor steps the air
    down. `prepareClouds(atmospheres)` makes all of it at once for a still.
  - Memory, in `clouds`: three `rgba16float` targets, about 12.4 MiB at 1080p, and 2 KiB a
    condensate.
  - Leans: `VOLUME_STEPS` 32 and `TEMPORAL_WEIGHT` 1/8. The sky's light is δ-Eddington's at
    τ_in and 0.6, held to the march's opacity, at most 4×.
- **The kernel, changed by T7.d's check (2026-11-18).**
  - The octaves' a^i now stands on the sun's path alone, with one view transmittance, as in
    Hillaire 2016. A grazing view had been 3× bright. The octaves alone now miss δ-Eddington by up
    to 94%, and K reaches about 27 at τ 50.
  - `cloud_volume_march_between` and `marchDeckRay` take `share`. The pass marches the covered part
    at τ_in and weighs it by f, the layer's independent-pixel form.
- **Not built (lean, 2026-11-18):** the 128³, 32³ and 128² noises and the transmittance map. The
  density is uniform through the deck, so the map would give the layer's tap, e^(−τ ÷ μ₀), which
  the terrain keeps.
