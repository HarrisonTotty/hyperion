# R11.T10.b: records as built

The as-built records of R11.T10.b, moved from the Risks and open points of
[11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md](../../11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **R11.T10.b's job, as built (2026-11-14); its render hook is not.**
  - `stills/stillJob.ts`: `runStillJob(options)` → `StillJobOutcome` (`saved`, `not-saved` or
    `lost` with `batchUnits`) and `StillSession` (`request()`, a store of `StillControlState`),
    over the interfaces `StillDevice`, `StillRenderer`, `StillSource` and `StillScene`.
  - Also there: `assembleTiles` (crops the padded column; alpha forced to 255, since R07 keeps
    `METER_CLASS` in the HDR alpha), `encodeStillPng`, `STILL_STREAMING_TIMEOUT_S` (20) and
    `STILL_PROGRESS_INTERVAL_MS` (250).
  - Order: `prepare()` → the streaming wait → five empty submits without timestamps → batches, one
    an animation frame, each after the last's `onSubmittedWorkDone()` → every tile read back.
  - The 20 s timeout starts once the pipelines are made, not at the request (lean, 2026-11-14).
  - A loss in flight abandons the job. `available` goes to `suspended`, and every later still that
    session is capped at max(1, ⌊n ÷ 2⌋) units; `suspended` goes to `disabled`.
  - A failure the loss reports within an animation frame counts as the loss. A loss while no
    still runs, or after the read-back, changes nothing.
  - VIEW: `ViewDisplay`'s `stillSource` prop is absent in the client. `useViewStills` in
    `ViewPanels` keeps the session through scenes; one session for each mounted display, and only
    VIEW is to get a source (lean, 2026-11-14).
  - The `STILLS` panel and fold stand only with a source, which replaces T10.c's held-back
    `TAKE STILL` (the UX review). The stills' lines stand under the fold row and MAXIMIZE VIEW.
  - Left for the hook in `photoreal/renderer.ts` (after perf-long-tasks-2's wave 2): the frozen
    scene and its cells; tiles in R01's `createRenderTarget` through `tileProjection` and
    `tileFrustum`; pipelines in `prepare()` by `createMaterialAsync` and `createComputeAsync`.
  - Also the hook's: jittered accumulation (samples repeat pass labels), fixed exposure, bloom
    before read-back, `readTexture`, `onPassTimes`, R01's fault, splitting an `overBudget` pass,
    the synchronous-pipeline test and HDR "every texel finite".
  - Smoke part 28 runs the job through the mount on the page's engine: a stand-in renderer of flat
    colours into four `createRenderTarget` tiles, its pipeline by `createMaterialAsync`, each tile
    through `readTexture`, every texel checked.
