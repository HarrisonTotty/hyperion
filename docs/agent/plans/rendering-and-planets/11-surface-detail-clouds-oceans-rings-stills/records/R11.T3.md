# R11.T3: records as built

The as-built records of R11.T3, moved from the Risks and open points of
[11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md](../../11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **R11.T3, as built (2026-11-13).**
  - Every rock is held behind `ROCKS_VERSION` = 33 (rule B, `scatter/hold.rs`): below it
    `rocks_in_cell` places none, `rocks_near` finds none and `surface_height_with_rocks` is R10's
    ground. 33's bump deletes what the tripwire `the_rocks_are_held_only_until_the_33_bump` lists
    and re-blesses the scatter goldens' headers alone.
  - The sketch's `RockError` is `FindRocksError`, with `Query(QueryHeightError)` alone.
    `RockCellKey` wraps a `PatchKey` (`new`, `containing(figure, octave, dir)`), and its
    `object_key(instance: u16)` cannot fail.
  - `ROCK_OCTAVE_LEVELS` is `rock_octave_levels(figure)`, the body's levels. A cell's k is its site
    cell's, read at the centre of the ancestor at the largest octave's level.
  - `rocks_near` and `surface_height_with_rocks` take `at: &[f64; 3]`. `Rock` gives `dir`,
    `base_centre`, `ground_height`, `diameter`, `shape`, `semi_axes`, `exposed_height`, `burial`,
    `reach`, `top_above(figure, at)` and `contact_bound(figure)`.
  - `with_rock_rules` and `with_rocks_placed` are `cfg(test)` alone, since the fitting crate links
    `hyperion-surface/testing` into release builds; the bench's `rocks_in_cell_at` is `testing`'s.
  - `ExtraLayer` is still empty: R11.T4.a gives it `Rocks(Vec<Rock>)` and writes `RockBakeHook`.
  - Design note 4, as built: k_ejecta ends at the crater's reach, (1 + a) R at most 2.54 R, not
    3R, since the field lists a crater only in the cells its reach touches; it is aged by A's
    decaying part e^(−t ÷ τ) alone, and the crater whose term is largest is the site's. `Melt`
    bears no rocks either, and a playa's salts have k_base 0, so only ejecta stand there.
  - A rock's frame (its up and horizontal axes) is private: T4's mesh needs a `Rock::frame()`, or
    the pole switch at |ν_z| ≥ 0.9 that `shape.rs` uses and `contact.rs`'s tests repeat.
  - Outside this crate's tests no rock is placed until 33's bump, since the switch is
    `cfg(test)` alone. T4's checks that need rocks run where a switch reaches them or after the
    bump, never through the `testing` feature (lean, 2026-11-13).
- **R11.T3, the hold folded at 33 (2026-11-18, train 33).**
  - `scatter::hold` is deleted (`ROCKS_VERSION`, `RockRules`, `with_rock_rules`,
    `with_rocks_placed`): `rocks_in_cell`, `rocks_near` and `surface_height_with_rocks` place the
    rocks with no switch, and their tests run as the version does.
  - The sim's `SURFACE_HOLDS` is empty, and smart-ci's `WASM_SKY_SOURCES` no longer names
    `scatter/hold`. The scatter goldens' values hold at 33; their headers moved alone.
