# R11.T6: records as built

The as-built records of R11.T6, moved from the Risks and open points of
[11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md](../../11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **R11.T6.a, as built (2026-11-18).**
  - Client, `view/surface/scatter/`: `pebbleLaw.ts` (the law, octaves, lattice, a cell's draws),
    `pebblePlace.ts` (`pebbles.wgsl`'s `PEBBLE_KERNELS` a vertex path, its records,
    `scatterEntries`, the CPU twin `placePebblesCpu`), `pebbleMaterial.ts` (`pebbleDraw.wgsl` over
    the lit terrain's source, `PEBBLE_MATERIALS`, `pebbleTemplates`), `scatterCull.ts` and
    `scatterCull.wgsl` (`intersectsBody`, `nearGround`, `craftBox`, `hullBox`), `scatterKernels.ts`
    (`ScatterKernelSet`, `scatterDrawn`) and `scatterPass.ts` (`ScatterPass`).
  - The pebbles draw through R14.T5.d's `ParticlePath` (its constructor now takes a memory
    category: `scatter`); the record's colour word is the patch place (2 × 12 bits) and a tint byte,
    its tag the frame's patch record (24 bits) and the fade's byte above it. Pass labels `scatter` and `scatter cull` (PASS_ROWS `scatter`);
    the draws ride the bodies pass after the terrain's draws that never discard, and the visibility
    buffer's forward draws.
  - k per palette slot comes from the surface crate: `WorkerFields::rock_abundance(body, material,
key)` over `scatter::material_site(field, class, entry, dir)` (site_at split, bit for bit), the
    wasm `rockAbundances(system, body, palette, face, level, i, j)`, carried as
    `TerrainLayers.rockAbundances` and written to each slot law's word 7 (`packClassLaws`'
    `abundances`). Also `authoritativeRockDiameterM()` and `rockNumberBetweenPerM2(k, low, high)`.
  - Wiring: the session makes one `ScatterKernelSet` after its materials (high only; none at a
    density of 0 or under `GeometrySettings.scatter` `off`) and gives lit passes
    `TerrainPassOptions.scatter` (with `scatterDensity`); `TerrainStepInput.scatterBodies` from
    `sceneScatterBoxes`; `TerrainFrameInput.decoration` from `LiveFrameTerrainInput.floorStepped`;
    `TerrainViewFrame.decorated` while it scatters over a patch whose bake bears pebbles.
  - Leans (2026-11-18): octaves 0.0125–0.4 m; ejecta's k at the patch centre; one shape (b/a √2/2,
    h/D 0.375, half buried) tinted 0.75–1.25; fade 4 → 2 px by contrast (whole size; normal and
    tint go over to the ground's; a fade byte of 0 is not placed); repose 30–40°; whole patches of
    the finest three levels only, morph by distance without the coarse–fine holds; no cascade
    casting and no thermal emission.
  - Deviation: one placement dispatch a view-frame over every scattered patch, not one a patch:
    entries of 16² cells, at most `MAX_SCATTER_PATCHES` 1,024 patches and `MAX_PEBBLE_ENTRIES`
    32,768 entries, nearest first, into `PEBBLE_CAPACITY` 2²⁰ × density instances.
  - Deviation: the patches are taken only while `expectedPebblesBound` (π r² of the bounding
    sphere, the largest k, each octave's least D) stays within `PEBBLE_BUDGET_SHARE` 0.9 of the
    capacity, so overflow, which would keep pebbles in the atomics' order, all but never happens.
  - Deviation: the cull runs inside the placement (`scatter_box_meets`), not as a pass of its own:
    at most 8 boxes (`MAX_SCATTER_BODIES`), nearest first; "within 0.2 m of the ground" is read at
    the ground under the box's centre (`nearGround`).
  - Deviation: the `scatter` row times the placement and the instance cull; the draws ride the
    bodies pass, so the draw cost shows only as the Mac pair's A − B.
  - `TerrainViewFrame.decorated` is true where the frame dispatched over a pebble-bearing patch, not
    where a pebble was drawn (no readback).
