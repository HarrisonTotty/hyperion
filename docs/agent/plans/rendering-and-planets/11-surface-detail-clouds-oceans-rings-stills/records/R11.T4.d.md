- **R11.T4.d, as built (2026-11-18).**
  - `couldTouchContacts(bodies, sweepS, surface)`: a third argument, `SweptSurface` (the figure,
    the ground under a point, the finest patch's edge and a cap on the patches forced).
    `SweptBody { radiusM, path }`, the path `extrapolated` (pose, velocity, gravity or `null`) or
    `planned` (positions; the view's poses carry no times, so a plan is swept whole).
  - The widening (lean, 2026-11-18): `extrapolationMargin(g, t)` is ½ g t², but the chain is widened
    by its ground share, `sweptWideningM` = m · min(1, (|p(t) − p(0)| + m) ÷ c + 2δ): gravity pulls
    along the vertical, so the ground beneath the arc is the line's but for the pull's turn from the
    normal. ½ g t² as a ground radius would force about 3 × 10³ finest patches at 30 s on a Moon
    and 10⁵ on an Earth. The arc test holds the ground beneath an integrated central-gravity arc
    (Moon, Earth) within it.
  - Where (lean): only the stretches where the body could be within its footprint and the reach of
    the ground (no higher than those and the fall, not yet through it).
  - Spacing (lean): every sample within 3 P − reach − s of a covering contact, contacts up to twice
    that apart, not one radius (some 800 contacts at 300 m/s, each selection looping over them).
    The forced region covers every could-touch disc at 2, 20 and 300 m/s, with and without g.
  - The cap: the chain stops where its forced patches would pass `maxForcedPatches`; the session
    gives ⌊`maxPatches` ÷ 2⌋. At the provisional 30 s horizon a fast skim's stretch passes it, and
    the guarantee holds only within the cap until R05.T18 measures the residency.
  - Session: `sceneSweeps` (descending craft, `isDescending`), chain appended to the pass's
    `grounded` (so held, forced, and its rocks drawn whole). Unwidened: the view's bodies hold no
    mass. A craft in the body's non-rotating frame sweeps with the ground's ω × r in its velocity.
  - Harness: `runFixedStep` gains `contacts`, `forcedLatencyS` and `onFrame`. The fast descent
    (600 m at 60 m/s to a 3 m flare and stop, forced bakes 30 s ÷ 20 each): 0 of 145 near frames
    with a patch under the craft not resident; 26 of 145 with its own contact alone.
  - Review fix (2026-11-18): in a body's turning frame (`SweptSurface.spinRadPerS`) the widening
    adds the Coriolis drift ω t² (v₀ + g t ÷ 3) and the centrifugal ½ ω² r t²; the arc test runs
    an Earth in its turning frame too. The session gives neither g nor ω.
  - NOT RUN (by hand): the plan's scripted descent onto a rocky plain, its rocks drawn on the low
    setting and in the wireframe and its frame time recorded.
