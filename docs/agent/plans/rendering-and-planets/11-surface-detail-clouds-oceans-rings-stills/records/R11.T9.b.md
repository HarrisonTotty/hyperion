# R11.T9.b: records as built

The as-built records of R11.T9.b, moved from the Risks and open points of
[11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md](../../11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **Deviations in R11.T9.b, as built** (2026-11-14).
  - _The module, for T9.c._ `rings/shadowingTable.ts` exports `SHADOWING_HEADER` (axes,
    `codesPerUnit`, τ nodes, `acceptance`, `checks`) and `SHADOWING_CODES`: f_e, the lit orders 2
    and 3 (I/F at ϖ = 1 ÷ μ₀/(μ + μ₀)) and the unlit ones (÷ μ₀) over (τ_phot, μ_eff, v), then
    q_e and q_o per face over (τ, μ). I/F = ϖ I_classical f_e + `rings::multiple_scattering`; the
    unlit single scattering stays classical.
  - _Axes (lean, 2026-11-14)._ μ_eff = 2μμ₀ ÷ (μ + μ₀), traced at B = B′; the phase as v = α ÷
    (π − 2B) lit and (π − α) ÷ (π − 2B) unlit; the node spacings are in the module's doc comment.
  - _The scattering point and the field (lean, 2026-11-14)._ Light leaves the point struck,
    mirrored in the plane normal to its direction where that looks into the sphere; the field is
    random sequential addition, largest first, at drawn heights, periodic in x and y.
  - _The order model (lean, 2026-11-14)._ With n = 3.09 the orders alternate, so the tail goes by
    parity, its ratios pooled over phase: worst node 14% lit and 24% unlit at ϖ 0.57; the μ_eff
    reduction at B ≠ B′ 9% lit and 27% unlit (the module's `checks`).
  - _Hapke's fallback (lean, 2026-11-14)._ 1 + 1 ÷ (1 + tan(α/2) ÷ h), B₀ = 1, D_eff = D₀ μ_eff;
    T9.a's `hapkeShadowHiding` is to match. |B_H − f_e| at most 0.96 (RMS 0.28) at α ≤ 30°, 0.50
    (RMS 0.14) where τ_phot ≥ 1: B₀ = 1 overstates a thin ring's opposition.
  - _The size range (Risks)._ 0.1–5 m: tilt ratio 1.183 at 6°, 1.093 at 0.5°; 1 cm–5 m (H_eff
    4.59 m): 1.113 and 1.095, so plan 14's radii would miss the 6° band.
  - _The α = 0.5° check_ gives 1.068 against "about 1.15 ± 0.05" (a finding); the slow test pins it.
  - _The slow test_ (`tests/ring_shadowing.rs`) runs the committed acceptance statistics on one
    thread, not a smoke size, and checks the committed header holds its figures.
  - _For T9.c._ f_e holds the interparticle opposition peak (2 at α = 0 in thick layers); Design
    note 14's surge must be the particles' own, or the peak is counted twice.
  - _Left undone._ Salo and French 2010's figures, Dones et al. 1993's n and Hapke 1986's h were
    not re-checked against their sources: the lane's pod had no web.
