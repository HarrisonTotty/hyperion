# R11.T7.b: records as built

The as-built records of R11.T7.b, moved from the Risks and open points of
[11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md](../../11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **The kernels, as built (2026-11-13; nothing drawn yet).** All under `clouds/`:
  - `cloudNoise.ts`: one 64³ `r8unorm` tile of Gaussianised gradient noise for every body
    (`CloudNoiseBuilder`, made a slice a step; `sampleCloudNoise`, `CLOUD_NOISE_STD` 0.974).
  - `cloudDensity.ts` and `shaders/cloudField.wgsl`: the shared τ field,
    `cloudFieldAt(coverage, noise, params, point, footprintM, octaveCap)` and
    `cloud_field_at(p, footprint_m, octave_cap) -> CloudSample`, with `cloudFieldParams` and
    `coverageTexels` (z_c, N and the wind a cell, `rgba16float`).
  - `shaders/cloudOptics.wgsl`: `cloud_delta_eddington(tau, g, co_albedo, mu0) -> CloudSlab`, the
    conservative form at 1 − ϖ′ ≤ 10⁻⁶ and the kμ₀ = 1 window ±5 × 10⁻³, as the notes rule.
  - `cloudLayer.ts` and `shaders/cloudLayer.wgsl`: `CLOUD_LAYER_MATERIAL`, `CloudLayers` (the noise
    tile and each body's rows and coverage, in the new `clouds` memory category),
    `packCloudLayer`, `cloudLayerUniforms` and `cloudLayerDraw`; `LAYER_OCTAVES` 12 high, 9 low.
  - `cloudShadow.ts` and `shaders/cloudShadow.wgsl`: `terrain_cloud_light(p, sun, source_radius)`
    and its twin `cloudLightAt`; `cloudProbe.ts`, the probe the smoke harness reads in `r32uint`.
  - `CLOUD_ENTRIES` in the catalogue, compiled in the smoke harness's new part 26 (25 left free).
- **One hook for both of R10's (lean, 2026-11-02).** `terrain_cloud_light` returns per source the
  direct factor (for `terrain_shadow_factor`), the sky factor and the diffuse share added to the
  sky, since each source crosses the layer at its own place; the wiring replaces
  `terrainLit.wgsl`'s `terrain_shadow_factor` stub with it and calls it per source.
- **Left to the wiring (wave 2):** the pass after each body's composite in `photoreal/*` and the
  built `clouds` slot; the decks carried from R08's assembly through the atmosphere cache; the
  terrain's cloudy lit forms behind `cloudShadows`, with the test that neither hook is written when
  it is off; R08's double count of a drawn deck (T7.a's records).
- **Pending: the owner's RTX and UHD 620 runs** of the layer's cost (about 1 ms, under 16 MB; it
  holds 256 KiB of noise and 256 KiB of coverage a body).
- **Deviations and leans (2026-11-02 and 2026-11-13).**
  - The in-cloud τ is linear in the excess z − z_c, a truncated normal, not Design note 10's
    log-normal: χ is 0.66 at C 0.5, 0.60 to 0.82 over C 0.05–0.95 (lean; T7.c's volume reads it).
  - The coverage's offset is solved again over the zonal field alone at each 0.25° step of the
    subsolar latitude (`CLOUD_SEASON_STEP_RAD`), never over the surveyed subset (lean).
  - The ground's sky factor takes T at the diffusivity's cosine 0.6, as the layer takes the sky; so
    the τ = 10 "within 2% of T_total" test holds while the sky is under about 8% of the beam.
  - Leans: the rain band at 0.4 of the subsolar latitude; ρ 0.7 an octave; ℓ₀ at most 1,000 km; the
    layer at its deck's base plus 0.25 of its depth; Earth's zonal pattern and winds on every world.
  - `SETTINGS.low.cloudShadows` is on, as the brainstorm's amendment of 2026-10-09 rules.
