# R11.T8.a: records as built

The as-built records of R11.T8.a, moved from the Risks and open points of
[11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md](../../11-surface-detail-clouds-oceans-rings-stills.md). A new record goes at the end, in the same form.

- **R11.T8.a, as built (2026-11-13).**
  - `ocean/seaState.ts`: `seaState(input)` returns `SeaState` (`glassy`,
    `slopeVariance.{upwind,crosswind}`, `significantHeightM`, `peakAngularFrequencyRadS`,
    `peakWavenumberRadPerM`, `spectrum`, `whitecapFraction`, `capillaryM3PerS2`); beside it
    `equivalentWind`, `generationThreshold`, `windAtHeight`, `spectralDensity`,
    `angularFrequencyOf`, `wavenumberOf`, `EARTH_SEA`, `SLOPE_VARIANCE_FLOOR`. `SeaAir` is the
    air's density and kinematic viscosity, which T8.b forms from the body's surface record.
  - `ocean/gerstner.ts`: `gerstnerSet(state, count, cutWavelengthM)` (the grid's cut a third
    argument), `summedSteepness`, `resolvedSlopeVariance`, `unresolvedSlopeVariance` (the BRDF's
    per-axis σ²), `gerstnerDisplacement`, `gerstnerJacobian`, `eulerianMeanHeightM`.
  - `ocean/liquids.ts`: `oceanLiquidOf(LiquidDto)` at the render channels, with `missing` and
    `approximate`; `fresnelReflectance`, `deepReflectance`, `remoteSensingReflectance`,
    `shallowReflectance`, `columnTransmittance`, `diffuseAttenuationPerM`, `refractedCosine`.
  - U_eq drives only Cox–Munk's slopes and the whitecaps; Pierson–Moskowitz's and JONSWAP's height
    and peak take the body's own wind (lean, 2026-11-13: at U_eq a 1 m/s Titan breeze raised a 6 m
    sea whose peak outran the wind sixfold). The identity at Earth's parameters is kept.
  - The threshold (lean, 2026-11-02): Donelan and Pierson 1987's input against viscous damping,
    1.74 m/s on Earth, 0.39–0.57 m/s on Titan's seas; a record without viscosity or tension takes
    Earth's, met by U_eq.
  - Absent members (lean, 2026-11-02): without absorption the column is opaque; without scattering
    R∞ is 0. The upwelling Q is π (lean). Each component's 1 − √(1 − k²A²) is split between the
    wind's axes as cos² and sin² of its angle from the wind (lean, 2026-11-02).
